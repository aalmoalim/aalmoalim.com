Virtue of Fasting Ramadan

1 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him: from the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, who said:

"Whoever fasts Ramadan with faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven. And whoever stands (in prayer) during the Night of Decree with faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven."(1)

(1) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (38/2014) and Muslim (760).

2 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, used to say:

"The five prayers, Friday to Friday, and Ramadan to Ramadan are expiations for what is between them, as long as one avoids major sins."(2)

(2) Narrated by Muslim (233).

3 - From Ka'b ibn 'Ujrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Come to the pulpit."

So we came. When he ascended the first step, he said: "Amin." When he ascended the second step, he said: "Amin." When he ascended the third step, he said: "Amin." When he came down, we said: "O Messenger of Allah, we heard something from you today that we had not heard before." He said:

"Jibril, peace be upon him, appeared to me and said: 'May he be distanced who reaches Ramadan and is not forgiven.' I said: 'Amin.' When I ascended the second step, he said: 'May he be distanced who is mentioned in your presence and does not send blessings upon you.' I said: 'Amin.' When I ascended the third step, he said: 'May he be distanced who reaches his parents in old age, or one of them, and does not cause them to enter Paradise.' I said: 'Amin.'"(3)

(3) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Al-Hakim (4/170).

4 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"When the month of Ramadan enters, the gates of heaven are opened, the gates of Hell are closed, and the devils are chained."(4)

(4) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1899, 2377) and Muslim (1079).

5 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"When the first night of the month of Ramadan comes, the devils and rebellious jinn are shackled, the gates of Hell are closed and none of them are opened, and the gates of Paradise are opened and none of them are closed. A caller calls out: 'O seeker of good, come forward! O seeker of evil, desist!' And Allah has freed ones from the Fire, and that is every night."(5)

(5) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Al-Tirmidhi (682).

6 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Ramadan has come to you, a blessed month. Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, has made fasting it obligatory upon you. In it, the gates of heaven are opened, the gates of Hell are closed, and the rebellious devils are shackled. In it, Allah has a night that is better than a thousand months. Whoever is deprived of its goodness is indeed deprived."(6)

(6) Narrated by Al-Nasa'i (2106) and Ahmad (2/230).

7 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Indeed, Allah has freed ones every day and night, and each of them has a supplication that is answered."(7)

(7) Meaning: in Ramadan.

8 - From 'Amr ibn Murrah al-Juhani, may Allah be pleased with him, who said:

"A man came to the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, and said: 'O Messenger of Allah, what do you think if I testify that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and that you are the Messenger of Allah, and I pray the five prayers, and I give zakah, and I fast Ramadan and stand (in prayer) during it, then who am I from?' He said: 'From the truthful ones and the martyrs.'"(8)

(8) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Ibn Hibban in his "Sahih" (8/223).
