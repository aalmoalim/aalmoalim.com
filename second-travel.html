Second: Travel

The people of knowledge have unanimously agreed that the traveler may take the concession of breaking the fast, and making up is required upon him, due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"So whoever among you is ill or on a journey, then [he should fast] a number of other days"
[Al-Baqarah 2:184]

And due to what 'A'ishah, may Allah be pleased with her, narrated: That Hamzah ibn 'Amr al-Aslami said to the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him:

"Shall I fast while traveling?" And he was one who fasted frequently. So he said: "If you wish, fast, and if you wish, break your fast."(1)

And consensus on this has been reported by al-Nawawi, Ibn Qudamah, Ibn Hubayrah, Ibn Hazm, and others.(2)

(1) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1943) and Muslim (789).

(2) "Al-Majmu'" (7/431), "Al-Mughni" (4/212), "Al-Ifsah" (1/412), "Maratib al-Ijma'" (1/40), "Bada'i' al-Sana'i'" (2/630, 631), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/464).
