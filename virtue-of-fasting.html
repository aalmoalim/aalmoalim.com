Virtue of Fasting

Many hadiths have been narrated about the virtue of fasting from the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him. We mention the following:

1 - From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, said:

"Every deed of the son of Adam is for him except fasting, for it is for Me and I will reward for it.(1) And fasting is a shield.(2) If it is a day of fasting for one of you, let him not engage in obscene speech(3) or raise his voice(4). If someone abuses him or fights him, let him say: 'I am a fasting person.' By the One in Whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, the breath of the fasting person is more pleasant to Allah than the fragrance of musk. The fasting person has two joys: he rejoices when he breaks his fast, and he rejoices when he meets his Lord because of his fasting."

This is the wording of Al-Bukhari. In Muslim's version: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Every deed of the son of Adam is multiplied: a good deed is ten times its like up to seven hundred times." Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, said: "Except fasting, for it is for Me and I will reward for it. He abandons his desire and his food for My sake. The fasting person has two joys: a joy when he breaks his fast and a joy when he meets his Lord. And the breath of the fasting person is more pleasant to Allah than the fragrance of musk."

In another wording from Al-Bukhari: "Fasting is a shield, so let him not engage in obscene speech or act ignorantly. If someone fights him or abuses him, let him say: 'I am fasting' twice. By the One in Whose hand is my soul, the breath of the fasting person is more pleasant to Allah, the Exalted, than the fragrance of musk. He abandons his food, drink, and desire for My sake. Fasting is for Me and I will reward for it, and a good deed is ten times its like."(5)

Explanation:

(1) Imam al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said in "Sharh Muslim" (8/29, 30): His statement, peace and blessings be upon him: "Allah, the Exalted, said: 'Every deed of the son of Adam is for him except fasting, for it is for Me and I will reward for it'" - the scholars differed regarding its meaning, despite all acts of worship being for Allah, Glorified and Exalted. It was said: The reason for attributing it to Allah, the Exalted, is that no one other than Allah, the Exalted, has been worshipped through it, for the disbelievers in no era have venerated any object of worship through fasting, even though they have venerated it through the form of prayer, prostration, charity, remembrance, and other things. It was said: Because fasting is far from showing off due to its hidden nature, unlike prayer, Hajj, fighting, charity, and other apparent acts of worship. It was said: Because the fasting person has no share for himself in it. Al-Khattabi said: It was said: That independence from food is from the attributes of Allah, the Exalted, so the fasting person draws near through what relates to this attribute, even though the attributes of Allah, the Exalted, have no resemblance. It was said: Its meaning is: I am the One who exclusively knows the amount of its reward or the multiplication of its good deeds, while other acts of worship have made some of His creation aware of the amount of their reward. It was said: It is an attribution of honor, like His statement, the Exalted: "the she-camel of Allah" even though the entire world belongs to Allah, the Exalted. In explaining this hadith is the greatness of the virtue of fasting and encouragement toward it. His statement, the Exalted: "and I will reward for it" is an indication of the greatness of its virtue and the abundance of its reward; for when the Generous One informs that He Himself will take charge of the reward, it indicates the greatness of the reward's value and the vastness of the giving.

(2) Al-Junnah: protection and covering.

(3) Yarfuth: What is meant by rafth here is obscene speech. It is used for this, for sexual intercourse, for its preliminaries, for mentioning it with women or absolutely, and it may include what is more general than that.

(4) Nor should he raise his voice: meaning: let him not do any of the actions of the ignorant such as shouting, foolishness, and the like.

(5) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1894, 1904, 5927) and Muslim (1151).

2 - From Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: I heard the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, say:

"Whoever fasts a day in the path of Allah, Allah will distance his face from the Fire by seventy autumns."(1)

(1) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (2840) and Muslim (1153).

3 - From 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Fasting and the Quran will intercede for the servant on the Day of Resurrection. Fasting will say: 'O Lord, I prevented him from food and desires during the day, so allow me to intercede for him.' And the Quran will say: 'I prevented him from sleep during the night, so allow me to intercede for him.' He said: 'So they will intercede.'"(2)

(2) Hasan Sahih: Narrated by Imam Ahmad (2/174) and Al-Hakim (1/740).

4 - From Hudhayfah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: I supported the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, against my chest, and he said:

"Whoever says 'There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah' seeking the Face of Allah, and it is sealed for him with that, he will enter Paradise. And whoever fasts a day seeking the Face of Allah, and it is sealed for him with that, he will enter Paradise. And whoever gives charity seeking the Face of Allah, and it is sealed for him with that, he will enter Paradise."(3)

(3) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Imam Ahmad (5/391).

5 - From Abu Umamah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: I came to the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, and said: "Command me with something I can take from you." He said:

"You should fast, for there is nothing like it."(4)

(4) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Al-Nasa'i (2220, 2222) and Ahmad (5/249).

6 - From Abu Umamah al-Bahili, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, who said:

"Whoever fasts a day in the path of Allah, Allah will place between him and the Fire a trench as wide as between the heaven and the earth."(5)

(5) Hasan Sahih: Narrated by Al-Tirmidhi (1624).

7 - From Abu Musa, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: We were at sea, and while we were sailing, we had raised the sail and could not see any island or anything, when we heard a caller calling:

"O people of the ship, stop, I will inform you."

We stood looking but did not see anything. He called seven times. When it was the seventh time, I stood and said: "O this one, inform us what you want to inform us about, for you see our situation, and we cannot stop for it." He said:

"Shall I not inform you of a decree that Allah has decreed upon Himself? Any servant who makes his soul thirsty for Allah on a hot day, Allah will quench his thirst on the Day of Resurrection."

Abu Usamah, one of the narrators of the hadith, added: "You would not wish to see Abu Musa fasting on a day except that you would see him."(1)

(1) Hasan hadith: Narrated by Ibn Abi Shaybah (2/273) and others.
