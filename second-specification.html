Specification

The Malikis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis in the school held that specification of intention is required for every obligatory fast, which is that one believes that he will fast tomorrow from Ramadan or from making it up or from expiation or from a vow. If he does not specify, it does not suffice him. And if he intends an absolute fast or intends voluntary fasting, it does not suffice him.

They argued for the requirement of specification by the statement of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him:

"And each person will have only what he intended."(1)

This is apparent in requiring specification, because the foundation of intention is understanding its requirement from the beginning of the hadith: "Actions are only by intentions," and because it is an obligatory fast, specification of intention is required for it, like the circumambulation of visitation. So if one performs circumambulation intending farewell or performs circumambulation with an absolute intention of circumambulation, it does not suffice him for the circumambulation of visitation.

And because fasting is an act of worship added to a time, specification is required in its intention, like the five prayers. And because specification is intended in itself, specification suffices for the intention of the obligation in the obligation, and the required in the required.(2)

Imam Ahmad, in a narration, held that specification of intention for Ramadan is not required, so it is valid with an absolute intention, and with an intention of voluntary fasting at night, and with an intention of obligation in which there is hesitation.(3)

The Hanafis, regarding specification, divided fasting into two categories:

First Category:

Specification is not required in it: which is: performance of Ramadan, a vow whose time is specified, and likewise voluntary fasting. For voluntary fasting, it is valid with an absolute intention of fasting without specification.

This is because Ramadan is a standard - as the scholars of principles say - and it is restricted, not allowing anything else of its kind, which is fasting. So no other fasting is legislated in it, so it became specified for the obligation. The specified does not need specification. The specified vow is considered by the obligation of Allah, the Exalted, so each of them is fasted with an absolute intention, and with its foundation, and with an intention of voluntary fasting, due to the absence of competition, as al-Haskafi says.(4)

And every day is specified for voluntary fasting, except Ramadan, and the days in which fasting is forbidden, and what the legally responsible person specifies himself. All of that is specified and does not need specification.

Second Category:

Specification is required in it: which is: making up Ramadan, making up what one invalidated from voluntary fasting, fasting expiations of all types, and an absolute vow without restriction to a time, whether it is conditional or absolute. Because it does not have a specified time, it is not fulfilled except with a specific intention, cutting off competition.(5)
