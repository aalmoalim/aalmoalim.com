Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Balad (The City)

Introduction: Surah Al-Balad is a Makkan surah that addresses the human struggle in life. It emphasizes that life is a test and a struggle, and success comes through patience, compassion, and faith. The surah contrasts those who take the difficult path of righteousness with those who take the easy path of disobedience.

The Oath by the City

لَا أُقْسِمُ بِهَٰذَا الْبَلَدِ
"I swear by this city"
[Al-Balad 90:1]
وَأَنتَ حِلٌّ بِهَٰذَا الْبَلَدِ
"And you, [O Muhammad], are free of restriction in this city"
[Al-Balad 90:2]

Allah swears by Makkah, the sacred city. The Prophet (peace be upon him) was in this city when the surah was revealed. The oath emphasizes the importance of the message that follows.

Human Creation and Struggle

وَوَالِدٍ وَمَا وَلَدَ
"And [by] a father and what he begets"
[Al-Balad 90:3]

Allah swears by the father (Adam) and what he begets (all of humanity). This emphasizes that all humans face the same struggle and test in life.

لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ فِي كَبَدٍ
"We have certainly created man into hardship"
[Al-Balad 90:4]

We have created man in كَبَد (kabad) - hardship, struggle, and difficulty. Life is inherently challenging, and this is by design. The word كَبَد means:

  • Struggle: Life requires effort and struggle
  • Hardship: Challenges are part of human existence
  • Difficulty: Nothing comes easy in this life

The Reality of Life: Life is a test and a struggle. This is not a punishment but a reality of human existence. Success comes to those who struggle in the right direction - toward Allah and righteousness.

The Two Paths

أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ أَحَدٌ
"Does he think that no one will have power over him?"
[Al-Balad 90:5]
يَقُولُ أَهْلَكْتُ مَالًا لُّبَدًا
"He says, 'I have spent wealth in abundance'"
[Al-Balad 90:6]

Some people think that because they have wealth, no one can harm them. They boast about spending money, thinking it makes them powerful and secure.

أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّمْ يَرَهُ أَحَدٌ
"Does he think that no one sees him?"
[Al-Balad 90:7]

Does he think that no one sees him? This is a warning that Allah sees everything, and nothing is hidden from Him.

The Steep Path

وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْعَقَبَةُ
"And what can make you know what is [breaking through] the difficult pass?"
[Al-Balad 90:12]

The عَقَبَة (aqabah) is the steep, difficult path - the path of righteousness that requires effort and struggle.

فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ
"It is the freeing of a slave"
[Al-Balad 90:13]
أَوْ إِطْعَامٌ فِي يَوْمٍ ذِي مَسْغَبَةٍ
"Or feeding on a day of severe hunger"
[Al-Balad 90:14]
يَتِيمًا ذَا مَقْرَبَةٍ
"An orphan of near relationship"
[Al-Balad 90:15]
أَوْ مِسْكِينًا ذَا مَتْرَبَةٍ
"Or a needy person in misery"
[Al-Balad 90:16]

The steep path includes:

  • Freeing a slave: Helping those in bondage
  • Feeding in times of hunger: Providing for others when food is scarce
  • Caring for orphans: Especially those who are relatives
  • Helping the needy: Those who are in misery and poverty
ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْمَرْحَمَةِ
"Then he was of those who believed and advised one another to patience and advised one another to compassion"
[Al-Balad 90:17]

Then he was of those who believed and advised one another to patience and compassion. The steep path requires:

  • Faith: Belief in Allah and the Hereafter
  • Patience: Enduring difficulties with perseverance
  • Compassion: Showing mercy and kindness to others

The Steep Path (Aqabah): This is the difficult path of righteousness that includes:

  1. Freeing slaves and helping those in bondage
  2. Feeding others in times of hunger
  3. Caring for orphans, especially relatives
  4. Helping the needy and those in misery
  5. Believing in Allah and the Hereafter
  6. Advising one another to patience
  7. Advising one another to compassion

The Two Groups

أُولَٰئِكَ أَصْحَابُ الْمَيْمَنَةِ
"Those are the companions of the right"
[Al-Balad 90:18]

Those who take the steep path are the companions of the right - they will receive their records in their right hands on the Day of Judgment.

وَالَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِآيَاتِنَا هُمْ أَصْحَابُ الْمَشْأَمَةِ
"But those who disbelieved in Our signs - they are the companions of the left"
[Al-Balad 90:19]
عَلَيْهِمْ نَارٌ مُّؤْصَدَةٌ
"Over them will be fire closed in upon them"
[Al-Balad 90:20]

Those who disbelieved in Allah's signs are the companions of the left - they will receive their records in their left hands and will be in a fire closed in upon them.

The Two Groups:

  • Companions of the Right: Those who took the steep path of righteousness, helped others, believed, and showed patience and compassion
  • Companions of the Left: Those who disbelieved, ignored the needy, and took the easy path of disobedience

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Life is a Struggle

Life is inherently difficult and requires struggle. This is not a punishment but a reality of human existence. Success comes to those who struggle in the right direction.

2. The Steep Path of Righteousness

The path to success is steep and difficult. It requires helping others, especially the most vulnerable: slaves, the hungry, orphans, and the needy.

3. Faith, Patience, and Compassion

The steep path requires three essential qualities: faith in Allah, patience in difficulties, and compassion toward others.

4. The Two Outcomes

Those who take the steep path will be companions of the right (Paradise), while those who disbelieve and ignore the needy will be companions of the left (Hellfire).

5. Wealth is Not Security

Wealth does not provide real security. True security comes from faith, good deeds, and helping others.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Balad emphasizes that:

  • Life is a struggle and a test by design
  • Success requires taking the steep path of righteousness
  • The steep path includes helping the vulnerable: slaves, the hungry, orphans, and the needy
  • Faith, patience, and compassion are essential qualities
  • Those who take this path will be companions of the right (Paradise)
  • Those who disbelieve and ignore the needy will be companions of the left (Hellfire)

The surah calls believers to recognize that life is a struggle and to choose the steep path of righteousness, which requires effort but leads to eternal success.
