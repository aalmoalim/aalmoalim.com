Intention

This is because fasting Ramadan is an act of worship, so it is not valid except with intention, like all other acts of worship.(1) And due to the hadith:

"Actions are only by intentions."(2)

Abstention may be due to habit, lack of appetite, illness, or exercise, so it is not specified except by intention, like standing for prayer and Hajj.

Al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: No fast is valid in any circumstance except with intention, and the place of intention is the heart. Utterance with the tongue is not required, without disagreement, and it does not suffice in place of the intention of the heart, without disagreement.(3)

Al-Wazir Ibn Hubayrah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: They have agreed on the obligation of intention for the obligatory fast in the month of Ramadan, and that it is not valid except with intention.(4)

(1) "Hashiyat al-Dusuqi" (1/520).

(2) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1).

(3) "Al-Majmu'" (7/482).

(4) "Al-Ifsah" (1/383).
