That the Traveler Not Intend to Reside During His Travel for a Period of Four Days and Nights

The traveler must not intend to reside during his travel for a period of four days and nights (according to the Malikis and Shafi'is), more than four days (according to the Hanbalis), or half a month or fifteen days (according to the Hanafis).(1)

(1) "Al-Durr al-Mukhtar" (1/528), "Maraqi al-Falah" (231), "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (59), "Sharh al-Muhalla 'ala al-Minhaj" (1/257), and "Al-Rawd al-Murbi'" (1/89).
