Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Buruj (The Constellations)

Introduction: Surah Al-Buruj is a Makkan surah that was revealed during a period of intense persecution and torture of the believers. The surah tells the story of the People of the Trench (Ashab al-Ukhdud) - a king who dug trenches, lit fires in them, and threw believers into the fire. The surah focuses on the main theme: the persecution of believers by the people of falsehood and Allah's promise of victory and protection for the believers.

Historical Context: To understand this surah, one must live the reality of the Companions - the persecution, torture, and oppression they faced. The surah was revealed to strengthen the believers during these difficult times and to warn the oppressors of their fate.

The Opening Oath

وَالسَّمَاءِ ذَاتِ الْبُرُوجِ
"By the sky containing great stars"
[Al-Buruj 85:1]
وَالْيَوْمِ الْمَوْعُودِ
"And [by] the promised Day"
[Al-Buruj 85:2]
وَشَاهِدٍ وَمَشْهُودٍ
"And [by] a witness and what is witnessed"
[Al-Buruj 85:3]

Allah swears by the sky containing great stars (al-burūj). The word بُرُوج (burūj) can mean:

  • The constellations or stations of the planets
  • Stars that shoot at devils to guard the sky
  • Fortresses or towers - indicating protection and guardianship

The Functions of Stars in the Quran: Scholars have identified three functions of stars mentioned in the Quran:

  1. Decoration: "And We have certainly beautified the nearest heaven with stars"
  2. Guidance: "And by stars, people are [also] guided"
  3. Protection: Stars that shoot at devils to guard the sky from those who try to steal the revelation

Just as Allah placed stars in the sky for decoration, guidance, and protection, He also places scholars and people of knowledge on earth who serve the same functions: they beautify the earth, guide people, and protect the revelation from distortion.

The word "burūj" (constellations/fortresses) indicates that Allah chooses men to carry this religion - they are like fortresses that protect the revelation, guide people, and beautify the earth. When they are absent, the earth becomes corrupt.

The Story: The People of the Trench

قُتِلَ أَصْحَابُ الْأُخْدُودِ
"Cursed are the companions of the trench"
[Al-Buruj 85:4]

The word قُتِلَ (qutila) is a form of curse. أَصْحَابُ (aṣḥāb) means companions - indicating they practiced this crime extensively, or they were the first to invent this method of torture. The word الْأُخْدُودِ (al-ukhdūd) means trenches - they dug trenches in the ground.

النَّارِ ذَاتِ الْوَقُودِ
"Of the fire, [abounding in] fuel"
[Al-Buruj 85:5]

The fire with fuel - notice it doesn't say "the kindled fire" but "the fire with fuel." This indicates that the people of falsehood constantly add fuel to keep the fire of fitnah (trial) burning. Whenever a fitnah weakens, they add new fuel - new ideas, new methods to mislead people.

The Constant Fuel: The people of falsehood don't want the fire of fitnah to die out. They constantly add fuel:

  • When one fitnah weakens, they create a new one
  • When one method of misleading fails, they invent another
  • They constantly work to keep people away from faith
إِذْ هُمْ عَلَيْهَا قُعُودٌ
"When they were [seated] around it"
[Al-Buruj 85:6]
وَهُمْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفْعَلُونَ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ شُهُودٌ
"And they, to what they were doing to the believers, were witnesses"
[Al-Buruj 85:7]

They were sitting around it, supervising it themselves. The leaders of falsehood don't delegate this work - they personally supervise the means of fitnah. The word قُعُود (qu'ūd) also indicates firmness and persistence - they sat for a long time, perfecting their methods.

And they were witnesses to what they were doing to the believers - they personally witnessed the torture. This shows their deep involvement and personal responsibility.

وَمَا نَقَمُوا مِنْهُمْ إِلَّا أَن يُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَمِيدِ
"And they resented them not except because they believed in Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy"
[Al-Buruj 85:8]

What did they resent them for? Only because they believed in Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy. The oppressors hate these two attributes of Allah:

  • Al-Azīz (The Exalted in Might): They want to be the ones with might and power, not Allah
  • Al-Ḥamīd (The Praiseworthy): They want to be praised, not Allah

The Complete Faith: The believers' faith was complete and mature - they understood the meaning of "Al-Azīz Al-Ḥamīd." This is why the oppressors hated them. If their faith had been weak or incomplete, the oppressors might have tolerated it. But complete faith that challenges their authority is what they cannot accept.

The Promise of Punishment

الَّذِي لَهُ مُلْكُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۚ وَاللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ شَهِيدٌ
"To whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. And Allah, over all things, is Witness"
[Al-Buruj 85:9]

Allah is the true King, and He witnessed everything they did. Even though they killed all the believers and left no witnesses on earth, Allah and the heavens witnessed their crimes.

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ فَتَنُوا الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ
"Indeed, those who have tortured the believing men and believing women"
[Al-Buruj 85:10]

Notice it mentions both believing men and believing women - the oppressors try to mislead everyone, men and women, young and old. They leave no one out.

ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا
"And then have not repented"
[Al-Buruj 85:11]

And they didn't repent - this wasn't a passing mistake but persistent enmity and insistence on falsehood.

فَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ الْحَرِيقِ
"Will have the punishment of Hell, and they will have the punishment of the Burning Fire"
[Al-Buruj 85:12]

They will have the punishment of Hell, and in addition, they will have the punishment of the Burning Fire - a specific punishment for those who burned believers. The punishment matches the crime - they burned believers, so they will burn in a special fire.

The Promise for the Believers

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتٌ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْكَبِيرُ
"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds will have gardens beneath which rivers flow. That is the great attainment"
[Al-Buruj 85:11]

For those who believed and did righteous deeds - even in times of fitnah, they continued to work for righteousness - they will have gardens beneath which rivers flow. This is the great victory.

The Real Victory: The word "victory" (fawz) is used here - even though they were killed and burned, they are the ones who truly won. The real victory is remaining firm on faith, not changing or compromising, even if it means death. This is the great victory.

Allah's Power and Promise

إِنَّ بَطْشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ
"Indeed, the vengeance of your Lord is severe"
[Al-Buruj 85:12]

Don't think that what the oppressors do to believers is comparable to what Allah will do to them. There's no comparison. Allah's vengeance is severe.

إِنَّهُ هُوَ يُبْدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ
"Indeed, it is He who originates [creation] and repeats"
[Al-Buruj 85:13]

He originates and repeats - He creates, causes death, then resurrects. The oppressors' punishment in this world is temporary, but Allah's punishment in the Hereafter is eternal. Or it can mean He begins one punishment and then repeats another - continuous punishment.

وَهُوَ الْغَفُورُ الْوَدُودُ
"And He is the Forgiving, the Affectionate"
[Al-Buruj 85:14]

Even after all this, He is the Forgiving, the Affectionate. The door of repentance is always open, even for those who tortured believers. This shows Allah's mercy - if they repent, He will forgive them.

ذُو الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيدِ
"Owner of the Noble Throne"
[Al-Buruj 85:15]
فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ
"Doer of what He intends"
[Al-Buruj 85:16]

He is the Owner of the Noble Throne - the true, lasting kingdom belongs to Him. He does whatever He wills. Nothing happens outside His will. Even the oppression is by His permission, as a test for the believers.

The Story of Previous Oppressors

هَلْ أَتَاكَ حَدِيثُ الْجُنُودِ
"Has there reached you the story of the soldiers"
[Al-Buruj 85:17]
فِرْعَوْنَ وَثَمُودَ
"[Those of] Pharaoh and Thamud?"
[Al-Buruj 85:18]

Has the story of the soldiers reached you - Pharaoh and Thamud? Both tried to prevent the signs from appearing by killing:

  • Pharaoh: Tried to kill the believers
  • Thamud: Killed the she-camel (the sign)

Both methods failed. You cannot prevent the truth from spreading by killing believers or by trying to destroy the signs.

بَلِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا فِي تَكْذِيبٍ
"But they [who disbelieve] are in [persistent] denial"
[Al-Buruj 85:19]
وَاللَّهُ مِن وَرَائِهِم مُّحِيطٌ
"And Allah is [fully] surrounding them"
[Al-Buruj 85:20]

They are in persistent denial, but Allah is surrounding them from behind. They think they're hidden, planning in secret, but Allah sees everything. It's like someone planning behind a curtain, thinking they're hidden, only to discover the curtain is transparent.

بَلْ هُوَ قُرْآنٌ مَّجِيدٌ
"But this is a glorious Qur'an"
[Al-Buruj 85:21]
فِي لَوْحٍ مَّحْفُوظٍ
"[Inscribed] in a Preserved Slate"
[Al-Buruj 85:22]

The surah ends with reassurance: This is a glorious Quran in a Preserved Slate. No matter how many believers you kill, as long as the Quran exists, new believers will emerge. The only way to stop the spread of faith would be to destroy the Quran, but that's impossible - it's preserved by Allah.

The Final Reassurance: The surah reassures the believers about the religion, not about their bodies. Your bodies may be killed, but the Quran is preserved. As long as the Quran exists, the religion will continue to spread and new believers will emerge.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Persecution is Expected

The people of falsehood will always try to mislead and persecute the believers. This is a constant struggle that will continue until the Day of Judgment.

2. The Fire of Fitnah is Constantly Fueled

The oppressors constantly add fuel to keep the fire of fitnah burning. When one method fails, they invent another. Believers must be aware of this and remain firm.

3. Complete Faith is What They Hate

They don't just hate any faith - they hate complete, mature faith that understands Allah's attributes and challenges their authority.

4. Allah Witnesses Everything

Even if all witnesses on earth are killed, Allah and the heavens witness the crimes. Nothing is hidden from Him.

5. The Real Victory

The real victory is remaining firm on faith, not compromising, even if it means death. Those who are killed for their faith are the true winners.

6. The Quran is Preserved

No matter how many believers are killed, as long as the Quran exists, the religion will continue. The Quran is preserved by Allah and cannot be destroyed.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Buruj addresses the reality of persecution that believers face. It tells the story of the People of the Trench to show that:

  • Persecution is a constant reality
  • The oppressors will be punished severely
  • The believers will be rewarded greatly
  • Allah witnesses everything
  • The Quran is preserved, so the religion will continue

The surah provides comfort and strength to believers facing persecution and serves as a warning to oppressors about their fate.
