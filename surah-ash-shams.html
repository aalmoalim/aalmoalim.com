Everything You Need to Know About Surah Ash-Shams (The Sun)

Introduction: Surah Ash-Shams is a Makkan surah that begins with a series of oaths by cosmic phenomena, then presents the fundamental choice facing every human: to purify or corrupt their soul. The surah uses the story of Thamud as a warning example of those who chose corruption.

The Opening Oaths

وَالشَّمْسِ وَضُحَاهَا
"By the sun and its brightness"
[Ash-Shams 91:1]
وَالْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَاهَا
"And [by] the moon when it follows it"
[Ash-Shams 91:2]
وَالنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّاهَا
"And [by] the day when it displays it"
[Ash-Shams 91:3]
وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَاهَا
"And [by] the night when it covers it"
[Ash-Shams 91:4]
وَالسَّمَاءِ وَمَا بَنَاهَا
"And [by] the sky and He who constructed it"
[Ash-Shams 91:5]
وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَاهَا
"And [by] the earth and He who spread it"
[Ash-Shams 91:6]
وَنَفْسٍ وَمَا سَوَّاهَا
"And [by] the soul and He who proportioned it"
[Ash-Shams 91:7]

Allah swears by the sun, moon, day, night, sky, earth, and the soul. These oaths emphasize the importance of what follows: the choice between purifying or corrupting the soul.

The Progression: The oaths move from cosmic phenomena (sun, moon, day, night, sky, earth) to the human soul. This progression shows that just as these cosmic elements follow Allah's design, the human soul should also follow Allah's guidance.

The Two Paths for the Soul

فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَاهَا
"And inspired it [with discernment of] its wickedness and its righteousness"
[Ash-Shams 91:8]

Allah inspired the soul with knowledge of its فُجُور (fujur - wickedness, corruption) and its تَقْوَى (taqwa - righteousness, piety). Every human knows instinctively what is right and wrong.

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّاهَا
"He has succeeded who purifies it"
[Ash-Shams 91:9]
وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّاهَا
"And he has failed who instills it [with corruption]"
[Ash-Shams 91:10]

He has succeeded who purifies it (زَكَّاهَا - zakkaha), and he has failed who instills it with corruption (دَسَّاهَا - dassaha).

The Two Paths:

  • Purification (Tazkiyah): Cleansing the soul from sins and developing it with good deeds and obedience to Allah. This leads to success.
  • Corruption (Dass): Burying the soul in sins and disobedience, hiding its natural inclination to good. This leads to failure.

The Story of Thamud as a Warning

كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِطَغْوَاهَا
"Thamud denied [their prophet] by reason of their transgression"
[Ash-Shams 91:11]

Thamud denied their prophet because of their transgression. They chose corruption over purification, and this led them to reject the truth.

إِذِ انبَعَثَ أَشْقَاهَا
"When the most wretched of them was sent forth"
[Ash-Shams 91:12]
فَقَالَ لَهُمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ نَاقَةَ اللَّهِ وَسُقْيَاهَا
"And the messenger of Allah [Salih] said to them, '[Do not harm] the she-camel of Allah or [prevent her from] her drink'"
[Ash-Shams 91:13]

When the most wretched of them (Qudar ibn Salif) was sent forth to kill the she-camel, the messenger of Allah (Salih) warned them not to harm Allah's she-camel or prevent her from drinking.

فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِذَنبِهِمْ فَسَوَّاهَا
"But they denied him and hamstrung her. So their Lord brought down upon them destruction for their sin and made it equal [upon all of them]"
[Ash-Shams 91:14]
وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقْبَاهَا
"And He does not fear the consequence thereof"
[Ash-Shams 91:15]

But they denied him and hamstrung the she-camel. So their Lord brought down destruction upon them for their sin and made it equal upon all of them. Allah does not fear the consequence of His punishment - He is just and powerful.

The Lesson from Thamud: Thamud chose corruption over purification. They rejected the clear sign (the she-camel) and killed it. Their destruction was swift and complete. This serves as a warning to all who choose corruption over purification.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Soul Has Two Inclinations

Every human soul knows instinctively what is right (taqwa) and what is wrong (fujur). This knowledge is inspired by Allah.

2. The Choice is Ours

We choose whether to purify our souls (leading to success) or corrupt them (leading to failure). This is the fundamental choice of life.

3. Purification Leads to Success

Those who purify their souls through obedience to Allah, good deeds, and avoiding sins will succeed in this life and the next.

4. Corruption Leads to Failure

Those who corrupt their souls by burying them in sins and disobedience will fail, as shown by the example of Thamud.

5. The Cosmic Order Reflects Divine Guidance

Just as the sun, moon, day, and night follow Allah's design, the human soul should follow Allah's guidance for purification.

6. Rejecting Clear Signs Leads to Destruction

Thamud rejected the clear sign (the she-camel) and were destroyed. This warns us not to reject Allah's clear guidance.

Conclusion

Surah Ash-Shams emphasizes that:

  • Every human soul knows right from wrong
  • We must choose between purifying or corrupting our souls
  • Purification leads to success, while corruption leads to failure
  • The story of Thamud serves as a warning against choosing corruption
  • Just as cosmic phenomena follow Allah's design, our souls should follow His guidance

The surah calls believers to purify their souls through obedience to Allah, good deeds, and avoiding sins, following the natural inclination toward righteousness that Allah has inspired in every soul.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Ash-Shams.
