Refraining from What Nullifies the Reward

Among the most important things that the fasting person should refrain from and beware of is what nullifies the reward of his fast from apparent and hidden sins. So he should protect his tongue from idle talk, nonsense, lying, backbiting, slander, obscenity, harshness, disputes, and argumentation. And he should restrain his limbs from all desires and forbidden things, and occupy himself with worship, remembrance of Allah, and recitation of the Quran. This - as al-Ghazali says - is the secret of fasting.(1)

In the two Sahihs from the hadith of Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: Allah, the Exalted, said:

"Every deed of the son of Adam is for him except fasting, for it is for Me and I will reward for it. And fasting is a shield. If it is a day of fasting for one of you, let him not engage in obscene speech or raise his voice. If someone abuses him or fights him, let him say: 'I am a fasting person.'"(2)

From Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Whoever does not abandon false speech and acting upon it, Allah has no need for him to abandon his food and drink."(3)

(1) "Al-Wajiz" (1/103) and "Al-Majmu'" (6/377).

(2) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1904) and Muslim (1151).

(3) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1903).
