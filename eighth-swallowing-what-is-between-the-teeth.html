Eighth: Swallowing What Is Between the Teeth

Ibn al-Mundhir, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The scholars have unanimously agreed that there is nothing upon the fasting person regarding what he swallows that flows with saliva from between his teeth, which he is unable to reject.

He said: If he is able to reject it and swallows it intentionally, Abu Hanifah said: He does not break his fast. And the rest of the scholars said: He breaks his fast. And with this I say.(1)

(1) "Al-Ijma'" (33) and "Al-Majmu'" (7/529).
