Foreplay

The jurists have agreed that whoever ejaculates during a day of Ramadan from direct contact less than the private parts, his fast is invalidated and making up is required upon him.

Then they differed regarding the obligation of expiation:

Abu Hanifah, al-Shafi'i, and Ahmad in one narration said: Expiation is not required, because it is breaking the fast without complete intercourse, so it resembles kissing. And because the principle is the absence of the obligation of expiation except in what the Shari'ah came with, and the Shari'ah came with requiring expiation in intercourse, and what is other than it is not in its meaning, because intercourse is more severe. And for this reason hadd is required by it in the property of another, and it is not required in what is other than it, so it remains on the principle. And if that reaches the authority, he punishes him, because it is forbidden, and there is no hadd or expiation in it, so ta'zir is established by it, like one who has direct contact with what is less than the private parts of a non-mahram woman. And because expiation requires the completeness of the transgression, because it is dropped by doubts, like hadds.

Imam Malik and Ahmad in the second narration said: Expiation is required upon him, because it is ejaculation from direct contact, so it resembles ejaculation from intercourse.(1)

(1) "Hashiyat al-Tahtawi" (1/437), "Tuhfat al-Fuqaha'" (1/358), "Sharh Fath al-Qadir" (2/331), "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/457), "Al-Ishraf" (202), "Al-Ifsah" (1/394), "Al-Majmu'" (7/529, 532), "Al-Hawi al-Kabir" (3/436), "Al-Insaf" (3/301), "Al-Mughni" (4/170), and "Al-Mudawwanah" (1/199).
