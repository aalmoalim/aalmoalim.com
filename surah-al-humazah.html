Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Humazah (The Slanderer)

Introduction: Surah Al-Humazah comes after Al-Asr, which showed that those who are preoccupied with competing in worldly increase (At-Takathur) or mocking and slandering others are in loss. This surah specifically addresses those who mock, backbite, and slander others - the "humazah" (backbiter) and "lumazah" (slanderer). The surah warns that those who accumulate wealth thinking it will make them immortal will be thrown into the crushing Fire (al-Hutamah).

The Opening: Woe to the Slanderer

وَيْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةٍ
"Woe to every scorner and mocker"
[Al-Humazah 104:1]

Woe to every scorner (هُمَزَةٍ - humazah) and mocker (لُّمَزَةٍ - lumazah). The word "woe" (wayl) indicates severe punishment. The "humazah" is one who backbites and mocks others, while the "lumazah" is one who slanders and defames others. These are intensive forms indicating that this behavior has become a constant part of their character.

The Two Types of Harm:

  • Humazah (Backbiter): Mocks and backbites others, often in secret or through gestures
  • Lumazah (Slanderer): Slanders and defames others openly, attacking their reputation and intentions
  • Both behaviors show contempt for others and disregard for their rights

The Cause: Accumulating Wealth

الَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالًا وَعَدَّدَهُ
"Who has collected wealth and [continuously] counted it"
[Al-Humazah 104:2]

Who has collected wealth and continuously counted it. This person's main occupation in life is accumulating and counting wealth - not for need, but out of greed and the belief that wealth will make them immortal.

يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ
"He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal"
[Al-Humazah 104:3]

He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal. This delusion leads to arrogance, which in turn leads to mocking and slandering others. The person believes wealth protects them from death and gives them power over others.

The Connection: The accumulation of wealth leads to:

  • Arrogance and pride
  • Belief that wealth makes one immortal
  • Contempt for others who have less
  • Mocking and slandering those who are weaker

The Punishment: The Crushing Fire

كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنبَذَنَّ فِي الْحُطَمَةِ
"No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher"
[Al-Humazah 104:4]

No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher (الْحُطَمَةِ - al-Hutamah). The punishment matches the crime - just as they crushed and broke others with their words, they will be crushed in the Fire.

وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْحُطَمَةُ
"And what can make you know what is the Crusher?"
[Al-Humazah 104:5]
نَارُ اللَّهِ الْمُوقَدَةُ
"It is the fire of Allah, [eternally] fueled"
[Al-Humazah 104:6]

And what can make you know what is the Crusher? It is the fire of Allah, eternally fueled. This is not an ordinary fire - it is Allah's Fire, which never goes out and crushes everything in it.

الَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى الْأَفْئِدَةِ
"Which mounts directed at the hearts"
[Al-Humazah 104:7]

Which mounts directed at the hearts. The Fire specifically targets the hearts - the source of arrogance, pride, and contempt for others. It burns through everything until it reaches the heart, causing the most intense pain.

إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌ
"Indeed, it [Hellfire] will be closed down upon them"
[Al-Humazah 104:8]
فِي عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ
"In extended columns"
[Al-Humazah 104:9]

Indeed, it will be closed down upon them in extended columns. They will be locked in Hellfire with extended columns - completely sealed with no escape, just as they sealed themselves off from mercy through their arrogance and mockery.

The Punishment Matches the Crime:

  • They crushed others with words → They will be crushed in the Fire
  • They targeted people's hearts with mockery → The Fire targets their hearts
  • They sealed themselves off from mercy → They will be sealed in Hellfire
  • They thought wealth made them immortal → They will face eternal punishment

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Mocking and Slandering Are Serious Sins

Backbiting and slandering others are not minor offenses - they warrant severe punishment in Hellfire.

2. Wealth Does Not Make One Immortal

Accumulating wealth and thinking it provides protection is a delusion. Death comes to everyone, regardless of wealth.

3. Arrogance Leads to Harming Others

When people become arrogant due to wealth, they begin to mock and slander those who have less.

4. The Punishment Matches the Crime

Those who crush others with their words will be crushed in the Fire. Those who target hearts will have their hearts targeted by the Fire.

5. Hellfire is Sealed

Those who mock and slander will be locked in Hellfire with no escape, just as they locked themselves out of mercy through their behavior.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Humazah emphasizes that:

  • Woe to those who mock and slander others
  • Accumulating wealth leads to arrogance and contempt for others
  • Wealth does not make one immortal - death comes to everyone
  • Those who crush others with words will be crushed in the Crushing Fire
  • The Fire targets the hearts - the source of arrogance and pride
  • They will be sealed in Hellfire with no escape

The surah calls believers to avoid mocking and slandering others, to use wealth properly, and to remember that wealth does not provide protection from death or punishment.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Humazah.
