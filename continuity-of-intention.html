Continuity of Intention

The jurists required continuity of intention. So if one intended to fast from the night then retracted from his intention before the appearance of dawn, he does not become a fasting person.

Al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Al-Mutawalli said: If one intended in the night then cut off the intention before dawn, its ruling falls, because abandoning the intention is the opposite of intention, unlike if one ate in the night after the intention, it does not become invalid, because eating is not its opposite.(1)

Al-Tahtawi the Hanafi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Continuity is required upon it. So if one intended from the night then retracted from his intention before the appearance of dawn, his retraction is valid and he does not become a fasting person. And if he breaks his fast, there is nothing upon him except making it up, due to the cutting off of intention by retraction. So there is no expiation upon him in Ramadan, due to the doubt of disagreement of those who required making the intention at night, except if he renews the intention, by intending the fast in the time of intention, to obtain it, because the first is not considered due to retraction from it.(2)

The intention does not become invalid by saying: "I will fast tomorrow if Allah wills," if one intended blessing or the occurrence of the fast and the continuation of life to its completion by the will of Allah, the Exalted, just as faith does not become invalid by saying: "I am a believer if Allah, the Exalted, wills."

Al-Qadi from the Hanbalis said: And likewise you say in all acts of worship: they do not become invalid by mentioning the will in their intention. That is: if one did not intend doubt or hesitation.

However, if one intended by "the will" doubt, such as doubting whether he will fast or not, or intended by it hesitation in resolve so he did not determine with the intention, or hesitation in resolve such that he hesitated whether he will intend the fast after that with determination or not, his fast is not valid.(3)

(1) "Al-Majmu'" (7/495).

(2) "Hashiyat al-Tahtawi" (354). See also: "Hashiyat al-Dusuqi" (1/528), "Al-Zarqani" (2/207), "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/449), and "Kashshaf al-Qina'" (2/316).

(3) "Al-Majmu'" (7/493), "Hashiyat al-Tahtawi" (354), "Kashshaf al-Qina'" (2/316), and "Al-Ifsah" (3/296, 267).
