Everything You Need to Know About Surah Ad-Duha (The Forenoon)

Introduction: Surah Ad-Duha was revealed to console and reassure the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) during a period when revelation had temporarily stopped. The surah reminds him of Allah's continuous care and blessings, and calls him to show gratitude by helping others, especially the orphan and the needy.

The Opening Oaths

وَالضُّحَىٰ
"By the morning brightness"
[Ad-Duha 93:1]
وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ
"And [by] the night when it covers with darkness"
[Ad-Duha 93:2]

Allah swears by the morning brightness (duha) and the night when it covers with darkness. These oaths emphasize the alternation of day and night, symbolizing that just as day follows night, ease follows difficulty, and Allah's care is continuous.

Allah Has Not Abandoned You

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ
"Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muhammad], nor has He detested [you]"
[Ad-Duha 93:3]

Your Lord has not taken leave of you, nor has He detested you. This directly addresses the Prophet's concern during the period when revelation had stopped. Allah reassures him that He has not abandoned or become displeased with him.

The Context: There was a period when revelation temporarily stopped, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) was concerned. This surah was revealed to console him and reassure him of Allah's continuous care and love.

Allah's Past and Future Care

وَلَلْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْأُولَىٰ
"And the Hereafter is better for you than the first [life]"
[Ad-Duha 93:4]

And the Hereafter is better for you than the first life. This reminds the Prophet that whatever he receives in this life is nothing compared to what awaits him in the Hereafter.

وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰ
"And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied"
[Ad-Duha 93:5]

And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied. Allah promises the Prophet that He will give him so much that he will be completely satisfied.

Allah's Past Blessings

أَلَمْ يَجِدْكَ يَتِيمًا فَآوَىٰ
"Did He not find you an orphan and give [you] refuge?"
[Ad-Duha 93:6]

Did He not find you an orphan and give you refuge? The Prophet was an orphan, and Allah provided him with care through his grandfather and uncle.

وَوَجَدَكَ ضَالًّا فَهَدَىٰ
"And He found you lost and guided [you]"
[Ad-Duha 93:7]

And He found you lost (seeking the truth) and guided you. Before revelation, the Prophet was seeking the truth, and Allah guided him to it.

وَوَجَدَكَ عَائِلًا فَأَغْنَىٰ
"And He found you poor and made [you] self-sufficient"
[Ad-Duha 93:8]

And He found you poor and made you self-sufficient. The Prophet was poor, and Allah provided for him through his marriage to Khadijah and later through the support of the Muslim community.

Allah's Past Care: The surah reminds the Prophet of how Allah cared for him in the past:

  • As an orphan: Allah gave him refuge and care
  • When lost: Allah guided him to the truth
  • When poor: Allah made him self-sufficient

This shows that Allah's care is continuous and will continue in the future.

The Call to Gratitude and Action

فَأَمَّا الْيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ
"So as for the orphan, do not oppress [him]"
[Ad-Duha 93:9]
وَأَمَّا السَّائِلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ
"And as for the petitioner, do not repel [him]"
[Ad-Duha 93:10]
وَأَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ
"But as for the favor of your Lord, report [it]"
[Ad-Duha 93:11]

As for the orphan, do not oppress him - show kindness to orphans, remembering that you were once an orphan. And as for the petitioner (one who asks for help), do not repel him - help those who ask for assistance. And as for the favor of your Lord, report it - speak about Allah's blessings and share the message of Islam.

Showing Gratitude: The way to show gratitude for Allah's blessings is to:

  • Help orphans: Do not oppress them, but show them kindness
  • Help those who ask: Do not repel petitioners, but assist them
  • Share Allah's blessings: Report and share the message of Islam

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Allah's Care is Continuous

Allah never abandons His servants. Even when it seems like He is distant, His care is continuous, just as day follows night.

2. Remember Past Blessings

When facing difficulties, remember how Allah has helped you in the past. This gives hope and reassurance.

3. The Hereafter is Better

Whatever we receive in this life is nothing compared to what awaits us in the Hereafter if we remain patient and grateful.

4. Gratitude Through Action

True gratitude is shown through actions: helping orphans, assisting those in need, and sharing Allah's message.

5. Personal Experience Builds Empathy

The Prophet's experience as an orphan made him more compassionate toward orphans. Our past experiences should make us more empathetic toward others.

Conclusion

Surah Ad-Duha emphasizes that:

  • Allah never abandons His servants - His care is continuous
  • We should remember Allah's past blessings to find hope in difficulties
  • The Hereafter is better than this life
  • Gratitude is shown through actions: helping orphans, assisting those in need, and sharing Allah's message
  • Personal experiences should make us more compassionate toward others

The surah calls believers to remember Allah's continuous care, show gratitude through helping others, and trust that ease follows difficulty, just as day follows night.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Ad-Duha.
