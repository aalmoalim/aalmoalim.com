Masturbation

The four imams have agreed that whoever masturbates with his hand has done a forbidden act, but his fast is not invalidated by it except if he ejaculates, then he breaks his fast without disagreement, and making up is required upon him, because it is ejaculation from direct contact, so it is like ejaculation from kissing. And because masturbation with the hand is like direct contact with what is less than the private parts of a non-mahram woman in sin and ta'zir, so likewise in breaking the fast.

However, they differed: is expiation required upon him or not?

The Malikis made it obligatory, and the majority (the Hanafis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis) did not make it obligatory.(1)

(1) "Tuhfat al-Fuqaha'" (1/358), "Al-Ikhtiyar" (1/141), "Al-Durr al-Mukhtar" (2/404), "Al-Tahtawi" (1/437), "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/457), "Al-Majmu'" (7/530, 532), "Al-Mughni" (4/169), "Al-Insaf" (3/301), "Al-Furu'" (3/38), and "Al-Fatawa al-Kubra" (25/224).
