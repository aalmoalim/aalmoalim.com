Menstruation and Postpartum Bleeding

The jurists have agreed that if a menstruating woman or a woman in postpartum bleeding menstruates or has postpartum bleeding, even in the last moment of the day, her fast is invalidated and making up that day is required upon her.

The four imams have agreed that if a menstruating woman's menstruation stops before dawn, and she intends to fast, her fast is valid, even if she delays the ritual bath until she wakes up or until the sun rises, because she is in a state of minor ritual impurity, and her impurity was removed before dawn, and nothing remains upon her except performing purification, so her fast is valid, like the one in major ritual impurity and the one in minor ritual impurity. However, on the condition that she intends to fast from the night.(1)

(1) "Al-Ifsah" (1/405), "Al-Majmu'" (7/419, 507), "Al-Mughni" (2/201, 205), "Sharh Fath al-Qadir" (2/371, 372), "Al-Mudawwanah al-Kubra" (1/184), and "Al-Ishraf" (198).
