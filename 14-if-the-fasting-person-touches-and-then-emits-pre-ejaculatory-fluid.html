Pre-Ejaculatory Fluid

The jurists differed regarding the fasting person if he touches or kisses and then emits pre-ejaculatory fluid:

Abu Hanifah and al-Shafi'i said: He does not break his fast by that, and his fast is valid, because it is a discharge that does not require ritual bath, so it resembles urine.

The two imams Malik and Ahmad said: His fast is invalidated and making up is required upon him, because it is a discharge that desire has entered, and it came out from direct contact, so it invalidates the fast like semen, and it differs from urine in this.(1)

From Looking

The jurists differed regarding the fasting person if he looks and then emits pre-ejaculatory fluid:

Abu Hanifah, al-Shafi'i, and Ahmad said: His fast is valid and he does not break his fast by that, because there is no text regarding breaking the fast by it, and it is not possible to compare it to the emission of semen due to its difference from it in rulings, so it remains on the principle.

Imam Malik said: He makes up a day in its place.(2)

(1) "Al-Taj wa al-Iklil" (4/40), "Sharh Mukhtasar Khalil" (2/253), "Al-Sharh al-Kabir ma'a Hashiyat al-Dusuqi" (2/159), "Tahbir al-Mukhtasar" (1/657), "Sharh al-Zarqani" (2/369), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/457).

(2) "Al-Taj wa al-Iklil" (4/40), "Sharh Mukhtasar Khalil" (2/253), "Al-Sharh al-Kabir ma'a Hashiyat al-Dusuqi" (2/159), "Tahbir al-Mukhtasar" (1/657), "Sharh al-Zarqani" (2/369), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/457).
