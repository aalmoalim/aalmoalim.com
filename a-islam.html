a - Islam

It is a general condition for being addressed by the branches of the Shari'ah. As for the disbeliever: if he is an original disbeliever, he is not required to do it in the state of disbelief because it is not valid from him. If he becomes Muslim, making up (qada') is not obligatory upon him, due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"Say to those who have disbelieved: if they desist, what has passed will be forgiven for them"
[Al-Anfal 8:38]

And because requiring the making up of what was missed in the state of disbelief would repel from Islam.

Similarly, the apostate, if he becomes Muslim, making it up is not required of him, and he is not required to perform it during his apostasy according to Abu Hanifah, Malik, and Ahmad.

Al-Shafi'i said: Making it up is required of him if he becomes Muslim.(1)

(1) "Al-Majmu'" (7/414), "Al-Bada'i'" (2/600), "Ahkam al-Qur'an" by Ibn al-'Arabi (2/399), "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (1/18), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/440). The details of this issue will come, if Allah wills, in the Book of Hajj.
