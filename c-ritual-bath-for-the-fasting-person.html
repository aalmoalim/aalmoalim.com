Ritual Bath for the Fasting Person

Al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: It is permissible for the fasting person to go down into water and submerge in it and pour it on his head, whether it is in a bathhouse or other than it. There is no disagreement in this,(1) due to what Abu Bakr ibn 'Abd al-Rahman narrated from some of the companions of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, who said:

"I indeed saw the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, at al-'Arj pouring water on his head from thirst, or from heat."(2)

From 'Aishah and Umm Salamah, may Allah be pleased with them both, who said:

"We bear witness regarding the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, that he would wake up in a state of major ritual impurity from intercourse, then perform ritual bath, then wake up fasting."(3)

(1) "Al-Majmu'" (7/577), and see: "Al-Mughni" (4/165), "Sharh Muntaha al-Iradat" (1/483), and "Ibn 'Abidin" (2/419).

(2) Sahih hadith: Previously mentioned.

(3) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1931), Muslim (781), and Ahmad (6/279).
