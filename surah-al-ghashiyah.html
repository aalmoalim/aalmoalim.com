Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Ghashiyah (The Overwhelming)

Introduction: Surah Al-Ghashiyah comes after Al-A'la, which called believers to purify themselves and prefer the Hereafter. This surah asks: "Has there reached you the story of the Overwhelming?" It describes the Day of Judgment and divides people into two groups based on their works. The surah emphasizes that the reward or punishment is from the same type as the work done in this world - the principle that "the recompense is of the same kind as the deed."

The Opening Question

هَلْ أَتَاكَ حَدِيثُ الْغَاشِيَةِ
"Has there reached you the story of the Overwhelming?"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:1]

The surah opens with a question: "Has there reached you the story of the Overwhelming?" The word الْغَاشِيَةِ (al-ghāshiyah) means something that covers or overwhelms. Scholars differ on what it refers to:

  • The Day of Judgment that overwhelms minds and hearts
  • Hellfire that covers the faces of the disbelievers

The Veil: The surah's name suggests that many people have a veil (ghishāwah) over their eyes in this world, preventing them from seeing the reality of the Hereafter. This surah comes to remove that veil and remind people of what they've forgotten.

The First Group: Humble Faces

وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ خَاشِعَةٌ
"[Some] faces, that Day, will be humbled"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:2]
عَامِلَةٌ نَّاصِبَةٌ
"Working [hard] and exhausted"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:3]

Faces that Day will be humbled - they refused to humble themselves before Allah in this world, so they come humbled on the Day of Judgment. The word عَامِلَةٌ (ʿāmilah) means working, and نَّاصِبَةٌ (nāṣibah) means exhausted.

Why "Working" on the Day of Judgment? Scholars explain that:

  • They will be forced to work - running, climbing mountains in Hellfire, trying to escape
  • They refused to work (worship) in this world, so they're forced to work in the Hereafter
  • They worked for disobedience in this world, so they work in punishment in the Hereafter

This is the principle: "The recompense is of the same kind as the deed." They refused work in this world, so they're forced to work in the Hereafter.

The Punishment: Food and Drink

تَصْلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةً
"They will [enter to] burn in an intensely hot Fire"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:4]
تُسْقَىٰ مِنْ عَيْنٍ آنِيَةٍ
"They will be given drink from a boiling spring"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:5]

Notice it says "they will be given drink" (tusqā) - not "they will drink" - indicating they're forced to drink. The word آنِيَةٍ (āniyah) means boiling hot. The smoke from this boiling water will scorch their faces even before they drink it.

لَّيْسَ لَهُمْ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعٍ
"For them there will be no food except from a poisonous, thorny plant"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:6]
لَّا يُسْمِنُ وَلَا يُغْنِي مِن جُوعٍ
"Which neither nourishes nor avails against hunger"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:7]

They have no food except from ضَرِيعٍ (ḍarīʿ) - a thorny, poisonous plant that animals avoid. It neither nourishes nor satisfies hunger. This is the food of those who refused to work for Allah in this world.

The Contrast: In this world, they enjoyed food and drink while plotting against believers and mocking them. Now, even their moments of "rest" (food and drink) become moments of torment. This shows that the punishment matches the crime - they enjoyed harming believers, so even their basic needs become torture.

The Second Group: Radiant Faces

وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاعِمَةٌ
"[Other] faces, that Day, will be radiant"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:8]
لِّسَعْيِهَا رَاضِيَةٌ
"Well-pleased with [the result of] their striving"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:9]

Faces that Day will be radiant - far from the Overwhelming, far from Hellfire, far from the distress of the Day of Judgment. They are pleased with their striving - when they look at their record, they're happy with what they did. They worked in this world, and now they're pleased with the result.

فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍ
"In an elevated garden"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:10]

In an elevated garden - this connects to Al-A'la (The Most High). Those who elevated themselves above worldly desires in this world are now in an elevated garden.

The Reward: Food and Drink

لَّا تَسْمَعُ فِيهَا لَاغِيَةً
"Wherein they will hear no unsuitable speech"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:11]
فِيهَا عَيْنٌ جَارِيَةٌ
"Within it is a flowing spring"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:12]
فِيهَا سُرُرٌ مَّرْفُوعَةٌ
"Within it are couches raised high"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:13]
وَأَكْوَابٌ مَّوْضُوعَةٌ
"And cups put in place"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:14]
وَنَمَارِقُ مَصْفُوفَةٌ
"And cushions lined up"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:15]
وَزَرَابِيُّ مَبْثُوثَةٌ
"And carpets spread around"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:16]

In contrast to the first group, the believers have:

  • No vain speech - they avoided it in this world, so they don't hear it in Paradise
  • A flowing spring - water flowing without channels, right before them
  • Raised couches - to rest after their work in this world
  • Cups placed - ready and waiting, with the perfect taste
  • Cushions lined up - for companionship and gathering
  • Carpets spread - beautiful rugs of all kinds spread around

The Principle: The recompense is of the same kind as the deed. Those who worked in this world rest in the Hereafter. Those who refused to work are forced to work. Those who avoided vain speech don't hear it. Those who gave up worldly pleasures are given eternal pleasures.

The Call to Reflect

أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى الْإِبِلِ كَيْفَ خُلِقَتْ
"Then do they not look at the camels - how they are created?"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:17]
وَإِلَى السَّمَاءِ كَيْفَ رُفِعَتْ
"And at the sky - how it is raised?"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:18]
وَإِلَى الْجِبَالِ كَيْفَ نُصِبَتْ
"And at the mountains - how they are erected?"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:19]
وَإِلَى الْأَرْضِ كَيْفَ سُطِحَتْ
"And at the earth - how it is spread out?"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:20]

After describing the two groups, the surah calls people to reflect on Allah's creation. This is to remove the veil (ghishāwah) from their eyes. The camel - closest to the Arabs - how was it created with all its abilities to live in the desert? The sky - how is it raised without pillars? The mountains - how are they erected to stabilize the earth? The earth - how is it spread out for us to live on?

فَذَكِّرْ ۖ إِنَّمَا أَنتَ مُذَكِّرٌ
"So remind, [O Muhammad]; you are only a reminder"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:21]
لَّسْتَ عَلَيْهِم بِمُصَيْطِرٍ
"You are not over them a controller"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:22]

So remind - your role is to remind them, to remove the veil, to call them to reflect. You cannot force them to believe - you are not a controller over them.

إِلَّا مَن تَوَلَّىٰ وَكَفَرَ
"However, he who turns away and disbelieves"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:23]
فَيُعَذِّبُهُ اللَّهُ الْعَذَابَ الْأَكْبَرَ
"Then Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:24]

However, he who turns away and disbelieves - Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment. Leave them to Allah - He will deal with those who reject after the reminder.

إِنَّ إِلَيْنَا إِيَابَهُمْ
"Indeed, to Us is their return"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:25]
ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا حِسَابَهُم
"Then indeed, upon Us is their account"
[Al-Ghashiyah 88:26]

Indeed, to Us is their return - they will return to Allah. Then indeed, upon Us is their account - Allah will take their account. Don't be impatient - even if you see them living in luxury in this world, they will return to Allah and be held accountable.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Overwhelming Event

The Day of Judgment or Hellfire will overwhelm everyone. Many people have a veil over their eyes in this world, preventing them from seeing this reality.

2. Two Groups Based on Works

People are divided into two groups based on their works, not just their beliefs. The recompense matches the deed.

3. The Principle: Recompense Matches the Deed

Those who refused to work are forced to work. Those who worked rest. Those who avoided vain speech don't hear it. Those who gave up worldly pleasures receive eternal pleasures.

4. The Contrast in Food and Drink

Even the moments of "rest" (food and drink) become moments of torment for the disbelievers, while they become moments of pleasure for the believers.

5. Reflection Removes the Veil

Reflecting on Allah's creation removes the veil from the eyes and reminds people of the Hereafter.

6. The Role of the Caller

The caller's role is to remind, not to force. Those who turn away will be dealt with by Allah.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Ghashiyah:

  • Reminds people of the Overwhelming Day they've forgotten
  • Describes the two groups based on their works
  • Shows that recompense matches the deed
  • Calls people to reflect on creation to remove the veil
  • Reassures that everyone will return to Allah for accountability

The surah serves as a wake-up call for those who have forgotten the Hereafter and prefer this world, reminding them that the Day of Judgment is real and that people will be divided based on their works.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) khutbah (sermon) of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Ghashiyah.
