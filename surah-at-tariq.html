Everything You Need to Know About Surah At-Tariq (The Nightcomer)

Introduction: Surah At-Tariq comes after Surah Al-Buruj, which ended with the reassurance that the Quran is preserved in a Preserved Slate. This surah continues the theme of protection - it swears by the protective stars in the sky and emphasizes that every soul has a guardian watching over it. The surah reassures believers that their deeds are being recorded and that Allah is capable of resurrecting them for accountability.

The Opening Oath: The Nightcomer

وَالسَّمَاءِ وَالطَّارِقِ
"By the sky and the nightcomer"
[At-Tariq 86:1]
وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الطَّارِقُ
"And what can make you know what is the nightcomer?"
[At-Tariq 86:2]
النَّجْمُ الثَّاقِبُ
"It is the piercing star"
[At-Tariq 86:3]

Allah swears by the sky in its vastness, then specifies "the nightcomer" (at-ṭāriq). The word طَارِق (ṭāriq) comes from the root meaning "to knock" - like someone knocking on a door at night. In the darkness of night, a bright star appears - the piercing star (an-najm ath-thāqib).

The Piercing Star: The word ثَّاقِب (thāqib) can mean:

  • Bright/Illuminating: It pierces through the darkness with its light
  • Piercing the Devils: It burns the devils who try to steal the revelation, as mentioned in other surahs: "Except one who snatches [some words] by theft, but they are pursued by a piercing flame" (As-Saffat 37:10)

Just as Allah placed bright stars in the dark sky to guide people and protect the revelation, He also places people of knowledge on earth who illuminate the path and protect the religion.

This surah comes after Al-Buruj to reassure believers about the complete protection of the revelation:

  • In the Preserved Slate (Al-Buruj)
  • During revelation (protected from devils by the piercing stars)
  • In the heart of the Prophet (peace be upon him) - as mentioned in the next surah (Al-A'la): "We will make you recite, [O Muhammad], and you will not forget"

Every Soul Has a Guardian

إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌ
"There is no soul but that it has over it a guardian"
[At-Tariq 86:4]

Notice it doesn't say "has a guardian for it" but "has a guardian over it" - indicating surveillance and recording. Every soul has a guardian watching over it, recording every action, every word, every glance, even every intention.

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said in a hadith qudsi: "When the servant intends to do a good deed but doesn't do it, Allah writes it as one good deed. If he intends it and does it, Allah writes it as ten good deeds. When he intends an evil deed but doesn't do it, Allah writes it as one good deed. If he intends it and does it, Allah writes it as one evil deed."

Even intentions are being watched and recorded. Nothing escapes Allah's knowledge.

Reassurance for Believers: This verse serves two purposes:

  1. Warning to oppressors: Your plots and schemes are being watched and recorded
  2. Reassurance to believers: Your good deeds, even your intentions, are being recorded and won't be lost

Reflection on Human Origin

فَلْيَنظُرِ الْإِنسَانُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ
"So let man observe from what he was created"
[At-Tariq 86:5]

Let man look at what he was created from - this is a call to reflect on human weakness and origin. This is one of the first truths mentioned in the Quran: "Read in the name of your Lord who created - created man from a clinging substance" (Al-Alaq 96:1-2).

خُلِقَ مِن مَّاءٍ دَافِقٍ
"He was created from a fluid, ejected"
[At-Tariq 86:6]
يَخْرُجُ مِن بَيْنِ الصُّلْبِ وَالتَّرَائِبِ
"Emerging from between the backbone and the ribs"
[At-Tariq 86:7]

He was created from gushing water (mā' dāfiq) - water that flows out, as if it has no control over itself. This weak human, created from a flowing liquid, emerges from between the backbone and ribs (the man's backbone and woman's ribs, or the place where the origin of blood is stored).

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: He spat in his hand and said: "O son of Adam, can you escape Me when I created you from something like this?"

How can this weak human, created from a drop of fluid, stand against Allah, the Lord of the heavens and the earth?

Allah's Power to Resurrect

إِنَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِ لَقَادِرٌ
"Indeed, He [i.e., Allah] is, to return him [to life], Able"
[At-Tariq 86:8]

Notice that death is not mentioned in the sequence. Why? Because death is inevitable - no one denies it. What people deny is the resurrection. So Allah emphasizes: "Indeed, He is able to return him" - to bring him back to life after death.

The Sequence:

  1. Created from gushing water
  2. Emerges from between backbone and ribs
  3. Allah is able to return him (resurrection)

Death is not mentioned because it's inevitable. The focus is on resurrection, which the disbelievers deny.

The Day of Secrets

يَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَائِرُ
"On the Day secrets are disclosed"
[At-Tariq 86:9]

On the Day when secrets are tested and revealed. The word سَرَائِر (sarā'ir) means secrets - either:

  • What people did in secret, away from others' sight
  • What people kept hidden in their hearts - thoughts, desires, hatred, plots

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "A banner will be raised for every traitor on the Day of Judgment, placed at his rear end, written on it: 'This is the treachery of so-and-so.'"

Everything done in secret will be exposed. Every plot, every scheme, every hidden thought will be revealed.

فَمَا لَهُ مِن قُوَّةٍ وَلَا نَاصِرٍ
"Then he will have no power or any helper"
[At-Tariq 86:10]

Then he will have no power (internal strength to defend himself) and no helper (external support). All the partners, allies, and supporters he had in this world will abandon him. Everyone will be concerned only with themselves.

The Second Oath: The Returning Sky and Cracking Earth

وَالسَّمَاءِ ذَاتِ الرَّجْعِ
"By the sky which returns [rain]"
[At-Tariq 86:11]
وَالْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ الصَّدْعِ
"And [by] the earth which splits [with vegetation]"
[At-Tariq 86:12]

Allah swears by the sky that returns (rain) - the water cycle: water evaporates, goes to the sky, then returns as rain. And by the earth that splits - when rain falls, the earth cracks open and brings forth vegetation and fruits.

The Connection: Just as:

  • Water comes from the sky and causes the earth to bring forth fruits and flowers
  • The Quran comes down on the hearts of believers and brings forth acts of worship and righteousness that wouldn't have appeared without it

This is the continuous cycle: The Quran is preserved, it comes down on new hearts, new believers emerge, they work for the religion, they may be killed, but the Quran continues to come down on other hearts, and the cycle continues.

The Quran is Decisive

إِنَّهُ لَقَوْلٌ فَصْلٌ
"Indeed, it [i.e., the Qur'an] is a decisive statement"
[At-Tariq 86:13]
وَمَا هُوَ بِالْهَزْلِ
"And it is not amusement"
[At-Tariq 86:14]

The Quran is a decisive statement - it separates truth from falsehood, believers from disbelievers, right from wrong. It's not amusement or play - it's a serious, weighty matter sent down by Allah.

The Continuous Plotting

إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًا
"Indeed, they are planning a plan"
[At-Tariq 86:15]
وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًا
"But I am planning a plan"
[At-Tariq 86:16]

They are plotting a plot - the disbelievers continue to plot against the believers day and night. But Allah says: "And I am plotting a plot" - notice that Allah confirms their plotting but doesn't need to confirm His own. We don't need confirmation that Allah is with us - that's certain.

فَمَهِّلِ الْكَافِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًا
"So give the disbelievers respite; give them respite for a little while"
[At-Tariq 86:17]

Give the disbelievers respite - wait for them, be patient. Some scholars say this means: "Wait until you have prepared and gathered strength, then fight them." Others say: "Be patient, their time is approaching."

The Command to Prepare: This verse came after the victory at Badr. It's a command to continue preparing and not to become complacent. The believers must always be ready and prepared to defend the religion.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Complete Protection of the Revelation

The surah reassures believers that the revelation is protected at every stage: in the Preserved Slate, during revelation (from devils), and in the heart of the Prophet (peace be upon him).

2. Every Action is Recorded

Every soul has a guardian watching over it, recording every action, word, glance, and even intention. Nothing is hidden from Allah.

3. Human Weakness

Humans are created from a weak, flowing liquid. How can such a weak creature stand against Allah, the Lord of the heavens and the earth?

4. Allah's Power to Resurrect

If Allah can create from nothing, He can certainly bring back to life. Resurrection is not difficult for Allah.

5. Secrets Will Be Revealed

Everything done in secret will be exposed on the Day of Judgment. Every plot, every scheme, every hidden thought will be revealed.

6. The Continuous Cycle

Just as water comes from the sky and brings forth vegetation, the Quran comes down on hearts and brings forth believers. This cycle continues - as long as the Quran exists, new believers will emerge.

7. The Plotting Continues

The disbelievers will continue to plot, but Allah also has a plan. The believers must be patient and prepared.

Conclusion

Surah At-Tariq continues the theme of protection and reassurance from Al-Buruj. It emphasizes that:

  • The revelation is completely protected
  • Every action is being watched and recorded
  • Humans are weak and cannot stand against Allah
  • Allah is able to resurrect for accountability
  • Secrets will be revealed
  • The Quran will continue to produce new believers
  • The believers must remain patient and prepared

The surah provides comfort to believers facing oppression, reminding them that their deeds are recorded, their oppressors' plots are known to Allah, and the religion is protected and will continue to spread.
