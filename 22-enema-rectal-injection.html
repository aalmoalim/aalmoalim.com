Enema

Al-ihtiqan (enema): Pouring medicine or inserting the like into the anus.(1) And it may be with a liquid or other than it:

The majority of jurists from the Hanafis, Malikis in the well-known view, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis held that enema with a liquid, from water - which is the common - or other than water, invalidates the fast and requires making up.

They explain that by saying that it reaches water to the cavity from an open passage, and that the non-customary is like the customary in what reaches, and because it is more comprehensive and more appropriate for requiring making up than nasal drops due to the invalidated obligation.

And due to the statement of Ibn 'Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both:

"Breaking the fast is from what enters, not from what exits."(2)

As for enema with a solid, there is some difference in it:

The Shafi'is and Hanbalis held that what enters the cavity from the anus by enema breaks the fast, because it reaches the cavity by choice, so it resembles eating.(3)

And likewise, the entry of the tip of a finger into the exit during cleaning breaks the fast. Imam al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: If a man inserts his finger or other than it into his anus, or a woman inserts her finger or other than it into her anus or vagina, and part remains outside, the fast becomes invalid by agreement of our companions.(4)

The Hanafis held that the disappearance of cotton and the like from dry solids invalidates the fast, and the absence of disappearance does not invalidate it, just as if its tip remains outside, because the absence of complete entry is like the absence of entry of anything at all, like inserting a finger that is not wet. As for what is wet with water and oil, it invalidates it.(5)

The Malikis specified breaking the fast and invalidation of the fast by liquid enema explicitly.

They said: Khalil specified with "liquid" to exclude enema with a solid, so there is no making up in it, nor in wicks that have oil on them due to their lightness.

In "Al-Mudawwanah": Ibn al-Qasim said: Malik was asked about wicks placed for enema?

Malik said: I see that as light, and I do not see anything upon him. Malik said: And if one uses enema with something that reaches his cavity, I see making up is required upon him. Ibn al-Qasim said: And there is no expiation upon him.(6)

The summary of the Maliki school regarding enema is four views:

One of them: It is the well-known, explicitly stated in "Mukhtasar Khalil": Breaking the fast by liquid enema.

The second: That enema breaks the fast absolutely.

The third: That using enema is disliked.

Ibn Habib, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Those who passed from the predecessors and the people of knowledge disliked treatment with enemas except from overwhelming necessity, where there is no alternative to treatment with them. So for this reason, making up the fast is recommended by using them.

The fourth: That it does not break the fast, and al-Lakhmi considered it good, because that does not reach the stomach, and there is no place from which what nourishes the body is distributed at all.(7)

And this is the choice of Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah, may Allah have mercy on him, and his statement in the previous issue has preceded.(8)

(1) "Al-Misbah al-Munir" under the root (haqn), "Maraqi al-Falah" (367), and "Al-Iqna'" (2/329). What is meant here is rectal enema.

(2) Narrated by Ibn Abi Shaybah in "Musannafuh" (2/308) and Al-Bayhaqi in "Al-Kubra" (1/116, 4/261). Al-Albani, may Allah have mercy on him, said in "Al-Irwa'" (2/377): Its chain is authentic, mawquf.

(3) "Al-Mughni" (4/157) and "Kashaf al-Qina'" (2/318).

(4) "Al-Majmu'" (7/517).

(5) "Maraqi al-Falah" (370). See: "Tabyin al-Haqa'iq" (1/329, 330), "Al-Durr al-Mukhtar" (2/102), "Mukhtasar al-Quduri" (63), and "Al-Ikhtiyar" (1/141).

(6) "Al-Sharh al-Kabir" (1/524), "Al-Mudawwanah" (1/197), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir ma'a Bulghat al-Salik" (1/462).

(7) "Mawahib al-Jalil" (2/424, 425) and "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (80).

(8) "Majmu' al-Fatawa" (25/234) and what follows.
