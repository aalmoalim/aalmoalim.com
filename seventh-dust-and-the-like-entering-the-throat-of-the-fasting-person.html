Seventh: Dust and the Like Entering the Throat of the Fasting Person

Ibn Hubayrah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: They have unanimously agreed that dust, smoke, or a fly, or a bug, if it enters the throat of the fasting person, does not invalidate his fast,(1) due to the inability to avoid it, and it is not possible to guard against it.

(1) "Al-Ifsah" (1/422).
