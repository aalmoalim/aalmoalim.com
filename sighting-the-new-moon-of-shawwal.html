Sighting the New Moon of Shawwal

The jurists have agreed that the new moon of Shawwal and all other months except the new moon of Ramadan is not established except with the testimony of two just men, due to what Husayn ibn al-Harith al-Jadali narrated:

That the governor of Makkah gave a sermon and then said:

"The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, instructed us to perform the sacrifice for the sighting. If we do not see it, and two just witnesses testify, we perform the sacrifice based on their testimony..."(1)

Al-Nasa'i narrated it from Husayn ibn al-Harith al-Jadali from 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn al-Khattab, that he addressed the people on the day in which there was doubt, and said:

"Indeed, I sat with the companions of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, and asked them, and they informed me that the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: 'Fast when you see it and break your fast when you see it and perform the sacrifice for it. If it is obscured from you, then complete thirty days. If two witnesses testify, then fast and break your fast.'"(2)

And due to what Rib'i ibn Hirash narrated from a man from the companions of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, who said:

"The people differed regarding the last day of Ramadan, then two Bedouins came and testified before the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, by Allah that they had seen the new moon yesterday evening. So the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, ordered the people to break their fast and to go to their prayer place in the morning."(3)

And by analogy to the rest of testimonies that are not about wealth, and wealth is not intended by them, and men usually have knowledge of them, along with the fact that there is no caution for worship in it, unlike the new moon of Ramadan.(4)

(1) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Abu Dawud (2338).

(2) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Al-Nasa'i (2116).

(3) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Abu Dawud (2339).

(4) "Al-Majmu'" (7/468), "Bidayat al-Mujtahid" (1/395), "Al-Mughni" (4/124), "Al-Bada'i'" (2/596), "Al-Mudawwanah" (1/174), "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (121), and "Al-Muntaqa" (2/39).
