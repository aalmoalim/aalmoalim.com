Definition of Fasting

Fasting (al-Siyam) in the Arabic language means: abstention (al-imsak), and it is used for every type of abstention. It is said: "he fasted" (sama) if he remained silent, and "the horses fasted" (samat al-khayl): they stopped. Allah, the Exalted, said to Maryam, peace be upon her:

"So if you see any human being, say: 'I have vowed a fast (sawman) to the Most Merciful, so I will not speak today to any human being.'"
[Maryam 19:26]

Ibn 'Abbas said: "silence" (samtan).

Abu 'Ubayd said: "Everyone who abstains from speech, food, or movement is fasting (sa'im)."

Al-Nabigha said:

"Horses fasting and horses not fasting... beneath the dust, and others chewing the bridles"

Meaning that the horses are divided into different types: some abstain from charging at the enemies, and some do not abstain.

Al-Khalil said: "Al-Siyam: standing without action, and al-Sawm: abstention from food. The horse fasted (sama al-faras), meaning: it stood without being fed. The day fasted (sama al-nahar sawman) when the time of noon arrived and it became balanced. Al-Sawm is the stillness of the wind. Al-Sawm: the verbal noun of sama yasumu sawman wa siyaman."(1)

Fasting (al-Sawm) in Islamic terminology: It is abstention from food, drink, and sexual intercourse, along with intention, at a specific time, for one who is addressed by it and is qualified for it.(2)

Al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "It is a specific abstention from a specific thing at a specific time by a specific person."(3)

Al-Sarakhsi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "It is an expression for a specific abstention: which is refraining from fulfilling the two desires: the desire of the stomach and the desire of the private parts.

By a specific person: meaning that he or she must be a Muslim, male or female, pure from menstruation and postpartum bleeding.

At a specific time: which is from after the appearance of dawn until the time of sunset.

In a specific manner: which is that it be with the intention of drawing near [to Allah]."(4)
