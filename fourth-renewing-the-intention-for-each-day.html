Renewing the Intention for Each Day

The people of knowledge have unanimously agreed that whoever intends to fast every night of the nights of the month of Ramadan and fasts, his fast is complete.(1)

However, they differed regarding one who intended on the first night that he will fast the entire month of Ramadan: does one intention suffice for the entire month of Ramadan, or is an intention necessary for each day?

The majority of jurists - the Hanafis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis in the school - held that each day of Ramadan requires an intention. So if one intended on the first night of Ramadan to fast the entire month, this intention is not valid for other than the first day, because the fast of each day is an independent act of worship whose time enters with the appearance of dawn and whose time exits with sunset. It is not invalidated by fasting the day before it, nor by the invalidation of the day after it. So one intention does not suffice, like prayers.

And due to the statement of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him:

"Whoever does not make the intention to fast from the night has no fast."(2)

The Malikis and Imam Ahmad in a narration held that one intention suffices for the entire month as long as he does not break it. So if one intended on the first night of Ramadan to fast all of it, it suffices him for all of it, and he does not need an intention for each day, due to the generality of the statement of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: "And each person will have only what he intended." And because it is one act of worship, so one intention suffices, like Hajj and the rak'ahs of prayer. And because he intended in a time whose kind is suitable for the intention of fasting, so it is valid, as if he intended each day in its night.

However, it is required that the continuity of fasting not be cut off by travel, illness, and the like, which cut off the obligation of continuity. If it is cut off by it, the one intention does not suffice. Rather, it is necessary to make the intention at night whenever one wants to fast, even if one continues fasting during his travel or illness. And similar to what preceded is if it is cut off by menstruation, postpartum bleeding, or insanity, then it is necessary to renew the intention, even if the impediment occurred after sunset and was removed before dawn.

However, it is recommended to make the intention every night in what one intention suffices for.

And likewise the ruling in every consecutive fast of making up, expiation, or consecutive vow, like one who vowed to fast a specific month, according to the previous disagreement.(3)

(1) "Al-Ijma'" (123) and "Al-Ishraf" (3/115).

(2) Sahih hadith: Previously cited.

(3) "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (80), "Al-Sharh al-Kabir" (1/521), "Bulghat al-Salik" (1/450), "Al-Ishraf" (1/195), "Al-Ikhtiyar" (1/160), "Al-Durr al-Mukhtar" (2/87), "Al-Majmu'" (7/482, 498), "Al-Mughni" (4/130), "Al-Ifsah" (1/384), "Al-Insaf" (3/293), and "Al-Furu'" (3/29).
