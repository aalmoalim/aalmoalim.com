Fourth: If the Fasting Person Wakes Up in a State of Major Ritual Impurity

The four imams have agreed that whoever wakes up fasting with the intention while in a state of major ritual impurity, whether from a wet dream or from intercourse, his fast is valid, even if he delays the ritual bath until after the appearance of dawn, with their recommendation for him to perform ritual bath before its appearance, due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"So now, have relations with them and seek that which Allah has decreed for you. And eat and drink until the white thread of dawn becomes distinct to you from the black thread [of night]. Then complete the fast until the sunset"
[Al-Baqarah 2:187]

And it necessarily follows that one wakes up in a state of major ritual impurity if one has relations until the appearance of dawn.

And due to what 'Aishah and Umm Salamah, may Allah be pleased with them both, narrated:

"The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, would wake up in a state of major ritual impurity, not from a wet dream, then he would fast."(1)

From 'Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her: A man came to the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, seeking a legal opinion, and she was listening from behind the door. So he said: O Messenger of Allah, prayer overtakes me while I am in a state of major ritual impurity. Should I fast? So the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "And I am overtaken by prayer while I am in a state of major ritual impurity, so I fast." So he said: You are not like us, O Messenger of Allah. Allah has forgiven you what has passed of your sin and what will come. So he said: By Allah, I indeed hope to be the most fearful of you of Allah and the most knowledgeable of you of what I avoid."(2)(3)

(1) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1825) and Muslim (1109).

(2) Narrated by Muslim (1110).

(3) "Al-Majmu'" (7/507) and what follows, "Sharh Muslim" (7/221), "Al-Ikhtiyar" (1/142), "Al-Mughni" (4/200), "Al-Taj wa-l-Iklil" (2/442), "Sharh Fath al-Qadir" (2/329), "Al-Ishraf" (1/198), "Al-Ifsah" (1/387, 405), "Al-Mabsut" (3/56), "Tuhfat al-Fuqaha'" (1/369), and "Bidayat al-Mujtahid" (1/404).
