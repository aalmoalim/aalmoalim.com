Everything You Need to Know About Surah At-Takathur (The Rivalry in Worldly Increase)

Introduction: Surah At-Takathur is a Makkan surah that addresses one of the greatest crimes humans commit: being distracted by competing in worldly increase until they visit the graves. Despite being only eight verses, this surah contains three oaths and three warnings (using "kalla" - "No!"), showing Allah's severe displeasure with this behavior. The surah warns that those who are distracted by accumulating wealth and competing with others will be questioned about every blessing they received.

The Crime: Distraction by Competition

أَلْهَاكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ
"Competition in [worldly] increase diverts you"
[At-Takathur 102:1]

Competition in worldly increase (التَّكَاثُرُ - at-takathur) diverts you. This refers to competing with others in accumulating wealth, children, property, and worldly possessions - not for need, but to boast and show superiority.

حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ
"Until you visit the graves"
[At-Takathur 102:2]

Until you visit the graves. This distraction continues until death - people remain preoccupied with accumulating and competing until they die and are buried. The word "visit" (zurtum) is significant: the time in the grave is just a visit compared to the eternity that follows.

The Distraction: People become so distracted by competing in worldly increase that:

  • They forget the purpose of life (worship and obedience to Allah)
  • They forget the Hereafter and accountability
  • They continue this distraction until death
  • They leave everything behind when they visit the graves

The Warning: "No!"

كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
"No! You are going to know"
[At-Takathur 102:3]
ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
"Then, no! You are going to know"
[At-Takathur 102:4]

No! You are going to know. Then, no! You are going to know. The repetition of "kalla" (No!) emphasizes the warning. You will know the consequences of your distraction - first in the grave, then on the Day of Judgment.

كَلَّا لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ الْيَقِينِ
"No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty"
[At-Takathur 102:5]

No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty. If you truly understood what awaits you, you would not be distracted by worldly competition. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "If you knew what I know, you would laugh little and weep much."

The Oaths and the Reality

لَتَرَوُنَّ الْجَحِيمَ
"You will surely see the Hellfire"
[At-Takathur 102:6]

You will surely see the Hellfire. This is the first oath - you will definitely see Hellfire with your own eyes.

ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ الْيَقِينِ
"Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty"
[At-Takathur 102:7]

Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty. Not just seeing it, but seeing it with absolute certainty - knowing it is real and that you are about to enter it.

ثُمَّ لَتُسْأَلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ النَّعِيمِ
"Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure"
[At-Takathur 102:8]

Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure (النَّعِيمِ - an-na'eem). You will be questioned about every blessing you received - health, wealth, time, opportunities - and how you used them. Did you use them in obedience to Allah, or were you distracted by competing in worldly increase?

The Three Oaths: Allah swears three times:

  • You will see Hellfire: The reality of punishment
  • You will see it with certainty: Absolute knowledge of its reality
  • You will be questioned about blessings: Accountability for every blessing received

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Competition in Worldly Increase is a Distraction

Competing with others in accumulating wealth, children, and possessions diverts people from the true purpose of life: worship and obedience to Allah.

2. The Distraction Continues Until Death

People remain preoccupied with worldly competition until they visit the graves - they never wake up to the reality of the Hereafter.

3. The Warning is Severe

The repetition of "kalla" (No!) three times shows Allah's severe displeasure with this behavior. This is a serious warning.

4. You Will See Hellfire

Those who are distracted by worldly competition will see Hellfire with their own eyes and know with certainty that they are about to enter it.

5. You Will Be Questioned About Every Blessing

Every blessing received - health, wealth, time, opportunities - will be questioned. How did you use it? Did you use it in obedience to Allah, or were you distracted by competing in worldly increase?

6. The Time in the Grave is Just a Visit

The word "visit" (zurtum) indicates that the time in the grave is brief compared to the eternity that follows in the Hereafter.

Conclusion

Surah At-Takathur emphasizes that:

  • Competition in worldly increase diverts people from the true purpose of life
  • This distraction continues until death - people never wake up
  • Allah is severely displeased with this behavior (three "kalla" warnings)
  • Those who are distracted will see Hellfire with certainty
  • They will be questioned about every blessing they received
  • The time in the grave is just a brief visit compared to eternity

The surah calls believers to wake up from the distraction of worldly competition, use their blessings in obedience to Allah, and prepare for the Day when they will be questioned about every blessing they received.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah At-Takathur.
