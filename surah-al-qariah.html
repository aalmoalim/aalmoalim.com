Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Qari'ah (The Striking Calamity)

Introduction: Surah Al-Qari'ah is a Makkan surah that begins with a powerful sound - the striking calamity (al-qari'ah). This surah was revealed to the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) while he was among the disbelievers in Makkah who rejected the Day of Resurrection. The surah presents the truth about the Day of Judgment in a way that captures attention and shakes hearts, describing the state of people and mountains, and the ultimate weighing of deeds.

The Opening: The Striking Calamity

الْقَارِعَةُ
"The Striking Calamity"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:1]

The surah opens with a single word: الْقَارِعَةُ (Al-Qari'ah - The Striking Calamity). This is a powerful sound that strikes the ears and hearts. The word "qari'ah" refers to a great sound that covers all other sounds - like the sound of war drums, calamity, or a devastating event.

مَا الْقَارِعَةُ
"What is the Striking Calamity?"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:2]
وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْقَارِعَةُ
"And what can make you know what the Striking Calamity is?"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:3]

What is the Striking Calamity? And what can make you know what it is? This question emphasizes that no matter how much we are told about it, we cannot fully comprehend its magnitude. It is beyond human imagination.

The Power of the Opening: The surah begins with a sound that captures attention - like a war drum or alarm. This sound makes people stop and listen, asking: "What is this? What does it mean?" The Prophet (peace be upon him) stood among disbelievers and recited this, making them wonder about this striking announcement.

The State of People and Mountains

يَوْمَ يَكُونُ النَّاسُ كَالْفَرَاشِ الْمَبْثُوثِ
"The Day when people will be like scattered moths"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:4]

The Day when people will be like scattered moths. On that Day, people who were once strong and powerful will become like moths - weak, scattered, and confused, flying in every direction without purpose or direction.

وَتَكُونُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ الْمَنفُوشِ
"And the mountains will be like tufts of wool"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:5]

And the mountains will be like tufts of wool (الْعِهْنِ الْمَنفُوشِ - al-'ihn al-manfush). The mountains, which are the heaviest and most solid things we see on earth, will become like colored wool scattered by the wind - light, dispersed, and without substance.

The Great Reversal: On that Day:

  • People: From strong and powerful to weak and scattered like moths
  • Mountains: From solid and heavy to light and scattered like wool
  • Everything that seemed permanent and stable will be overturned
  • Only deeds will remain stable and weigh on the scales

The Weighing of Deeds

فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَازِينُهُ
"Then as for one whose scales are heavy [with good deeds]"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:6]
فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ
"He will be in a pleasant life"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:7]

Then as for one whose scales are heavy with good deeds - he will be in a pleasant life. In a scene where everything is light and scattered, those whose scales are heavy with good deeds will find stability and contentment.

وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَازِينُهُ
"But as for one whose scales are light"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:8]
فَأُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ
"His refuge will be an abyss"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:9]

But as for one whose scales are light - his refuge will be an abyss (هَاوِيَةٌ - haawiyah, meaning Hellfire). Those whose deeds are light will fall into the depths of Hell.

وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا هِيَهْ
"And what can make you know what that is?"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:10]
نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ
"It is a Fire, intensely hot"
[Al-Qari'ah 101:11]

And what can make you know what that is? It is a Fire, intensely hot. Just as we cannot fully comprehend the Striking Calamity, we cannot fully comprehend the intensity of Hellfire.

The Ultimate Division:

  • Heavy scales: Good deeds outweigh bad deeds - pleasant life in Paradise
  • Light scales: Bad deeds outweigh good deeds - abyss of Hellfire
  • Every deed, no matter how small, will be weighed
  • The scales are just and accurate - nothing is hidden

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Striking Calamity Will Come

The Day of Judgment will come with a sound so powerful it will capture everyone's attention. This is certain and inevitable.

2. Everything Will Be Overturned

On that Day, people will become like scattered moths, and mountains like tufts of wool. Everything that seemed permanent will be overturned.

3. Only Deeds Will Matter

In a scene where everything is light and scattered, only deeds will have weight. Those with heavy scales (good deeds) will find stability, while those with light scales will fall into the abyss.

4. The Scales Are Just

Every deed will be weighed accurately. Good deeds will be rewarded, and bad deeds will be punished. Nothing is hidden or forgotten.

5. We Cannot Fully Comprehend

Just as we cannot fully understand the magnitude of the Striking Calamity, we cannot fully understand the intensity of Hellfire. This should make us fear and prepare.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Qari'ah emphasizes that:

  • The Day of Judgment will come with a striking calamity that captures all attention
  • On that Day, people will be like scattered moths and mountains like tufts of wool
  • Only deeds will have weight - those with heavy scales will be in a pleasant life
  • Those with light scales will fall into the abyss of Hellfire
  • We cannot fully comprehend the magnitude of these events, which should make us prepare

The surah calls believers to prepare for that Day by doing good deeds that will make their scales heavy, ensuring they will be among those in a pleasant life rather than those falling into the abyss.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Qari'ah.
