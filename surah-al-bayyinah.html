Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Bayyinah (The Clear Evidence)

Introduction: Surah Al-Bayyinah addresses the clear evidence that came with the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) - the Quran. It describes how the People of the Book (Jews and Christians) and the polytheists were divided until this clear evidence came. The surah then describes the ultimate division between the righteous and the wicked on the Day of Judgment.

The Clear Evidence

لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأْتِيَهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ
"Those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists were not to be parted [from misbelief] until there came to them clear evidence"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:1]

Those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture (Jews and Christians) and the polytheists were not to be parted from their misbelief until there came to them clear evidence. This clear evidence is the Quran and the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

رَسُولٌ مِّنَ اللَّهِ يَتْلُو صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةً
"A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:2]
فِيهَا كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ
"Within which are correct writings [i.e., rulings and laws]"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:3]

A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures - within which are correct writings. The clear evidence is the Messenger (Muhammad) reciting the Quran, which contains correct and straight guidance.

The Clear Evidence: The Quran is the clear evidence that:

  • Is recited by a Messenger from Allah
  • Contains purified and correct writings
  • Provides straight and clear guidance
  • Separates truth from falsehood

The Division After the Clear Evidence

وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا مِن بَعْدِ مَا جَاءَتْهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ
"Nor did those who were given the Scripture become divided until after there had come to them clear evidence"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:4]

Nor did those who were given the Scripture become divided until after there had come to them clear evidence. After the clear evidence (the Quran) came, people were divided into those who accepted it and those who rejected it.

وَمَا أُمِرُوا إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ حُنَفَاءَ وَيُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُوا الزَّكَاةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ الْقَيِّمَةِ
"And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:5]

And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion. The clear evidence commands pure worship of Allah, sincerity, prayer, and charity.

The Correct Religion: The clear evidence establishes that the correct religion requires:

  • Worshiping Allah alone: Pure monotheism
  • Sincerity: Being sincere to Allah in religion
  • Inclining to truth: Following the straight path
  • Establishing prayer: Regular worship
  • Giving zakah: Charity and purification of wealth

The Ultimate Division

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ شَرُّ الْبَرِيَّةِ
"Indeed, those who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists will be in the fire of Hell, abiding eternally therein. Those are the worst of creatures"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:6]

Indeed, those who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists will be in the fire of Hell, abiding eternally therein. Those are the worst of creatures. Those who reject the clear evidence after it has come to them are the worst of all creation.

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ
"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - those are the best of creatures"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:7]

Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - those are the best of creatures. Those who accept the clear evidence and act upon it are the best of all creation.

جَزَاؤُهُمْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّاتُ عَدْنٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۖ رَّضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُ
"Their reward with Allah will be gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever, Allah being pleased with them and they with Him. That is for whoever has feared his Lord"
[Al-Bayyinah 98:8]

Their reward with Allah will be gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever, Allah being pleased with them and they with Him. That is for whoever has feared his Lord. The believers will receive the ultimate reward: Paradise, where Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him.

The Ultimate Division:

  • The worst of creatures: Those who disbelieve after the clear evidence comes - they will be in Hell forever
  • The best of creatures: Those who believe and do righteous deeds - they will be in Paradise forever, with Allah pleased with them

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Clear Evidence Has Come

The Quran is the clear evidence that separates truth from falsehood. It came through the Messenger Muhammad (peace be upon him) and contains correct guidance.

2. Division After Evidence

After the clear evidence came, people were divided into those who accepted it and those who rejected it. This division is based on their response to the truth.

3. The Correct Religion

The correct religion requires pure worship of Allah, sincerity, prayer, and charity. This is what the clear evidence establishes.

4. The Ultimate Division

On the Day of Judgment, there will be a clear division: the worst of creatures (disbelievers) in Hell, and the best of creatures (believers) in Paradise.

5. The Reward of the Righteous

Those who believe and do righteous deeds will receive Paradise, where Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Bayyinah emphasizes that:

  • The Quran is the clear evidence that came through the Messenger Muhammad (peace be upon him)
  • After this clear evidence came, people were divided into believers and disbelievers
  • The correct religion requires pure worship, sincerity, prayer, and charity
  • Those who disbelieve after the evidence are the worst of creatures and will be in Hell
  • Those who believe and do righteous deeds are the best of creatures and will be in Paradise

The surah calls believers to recognize the clear evidence, accept it, act upon it, and become among the best of creatures who will receive the ultimate reward.
