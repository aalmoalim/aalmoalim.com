Injection in Urinary Passage

The jurists express this as "dripping" (al-taqtir), and it is according to this detail:

First: Dripping into the Male Urethra, i.e., the Penis

The majority of jurists - Abu Hanifah, Muhammad, Malik, Ahmad, and the Shafi'is in one view, and it is the choice of Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah - held that it does not break the fast, whether it reaches the bladder or does not reach, because the inside of the penis is not a passage to the cavity. Rather, urine exits filtered, so what remains in it does not reach the cavity, so it does not break the fast, like what remains in his mouth and he does not swallow it.(1)

Al-Buhuti, may Allah have mercy on him, said: If one drips into it, or inserts something into it and it reaches the bladder, his fast does not become invalid.(2)

As for the Shafi'is, al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: As for if one drips something into his urethra and it does not reach the bladder, or injects a catheter into it, there are three views:

The most correct of them: It breaks the fast, and the majority have stated this.

The second: It does not.

The third: If it passes the glans, it breaks the fast, otherwise it does not. And Allah knows best.(3)

Second: Dripping into the Female Genitalia

The Hanafis in the more correct view, the Malikis in their school, and what is taken from the statement of the Shafi'is and Hanbalis who explicitly mentioned only the urethra, held that the fast becomes invalid by it. Al-Kamal ibn al-Humam explained it by saying that it resembles enema.

And one view according to the Malikis: The gathering of two conditions:

One of them: That it is from the lower, wide passage.

The other: Enema with a liquid.

And al-Dardir, may Allah have mercy on him, explicitly stated breaking the fast by it, and al-Dusuqi explicitly stated the requirement of making up on the well-known view, and its opposite according to Ibn Habib is the recommendation of making up, due to enema with a liquid that reaches the stomach, from the anus or the female genitalia.

Just as al-Dardir explicitly stated that enema with a solid, there is no making up in it, nor in wicks that have oil on them.(4)
