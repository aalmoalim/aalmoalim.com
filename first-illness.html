First: Illness

Illness is: Everything by which a person departs from the limit of health due to an ailment.(1)

Ibn Qudamah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The people of knowledge have unanimously agreed on the permissibility of breaking the fast for the sick person in general.(2)

Imam al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: If a healthy person wakes up fasting, then becomes ill, it is permissible for him to break his fast without disagreement.(3)

The basis for it is the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"So whoever among you is ill or on a journey, then [he should fast] a number of other days"
[Al-Baqarah 2:184]

The four imams have agreed that the sick person who fears an increase in his illness by fasting, or delay in recovery, or damage to an organ, may break his fast and make it up. Rather, breaking his fast is recommended, and completing it is disliked, because it may lead to destruction, so it is necessary to avoid it.(4)

(1) "Al-Misbah al-Munir" under the root (marad).

(2) "Al-Mughni" (4/210).

(3) "Al-Majmu'" (7/423).

(4) "Al-Ifsah" (1/411), "Maratib al-Ijma'" by Ibn Hazm (40), "Sharh Fath al-Qadir" (2/350), "Al-Bada'i'" (630), "Mukhtasar al-Quduri" (63), "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/465), "Al-Majmu'" (7/423), and "Al-Mughni" (4/210).
