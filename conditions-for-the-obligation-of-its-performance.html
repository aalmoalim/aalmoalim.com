Conditions for the Obligation of Its Performance

The conditions for the obligation of performance—meaning clearing the liability of the legally responsible person from the obligation in its designated time—are:(1)

a - Health and Being Free from Illness

Health and freedom from illness are conditions for the obligation of performance, due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

“And whoever is ill or on a journey [then an equal number of other days].” (2)

b - Residence (Not Being in a State of Travel)

Residence is also a condition, based on the same verse: “And whoever is ill or on a journey [then an equal number of other days]” [Al-Baqarah 2:185].

Ibn Juzayy, may Allah have mercy on him, said: As for health and residence, they are conditions for the obligation of fasting, not for its validity, nor for the obligation of making up (qada'). For the obligation of fasting drops from the sick person and the traveler, and making up is obligatory upon them if they break their fast, by consensus. Their fasting is valid if they fast, contrary to the Zahiri school.(3)

c - A Woman Being Free from Menstruation and Postpartum Bleeding

It is a condition that the woman be free of menstruation and postpartum bleeding, because the menstruating woman and the woman in postpartum bleeding are not fit for fasting, due to the statement of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him:

“Is it not the case that when she menstruates she does not pray and does not fast?”(4)

And due to the hadith of ‘A’ishah, may Allah be pleased with her, when Mu‘adhah asked her: “What is the matter that the menstruating woman makes up the fast but does not make up the prayer?” She said: “Are you a Haruriyyah?” I said: “I am not a Haruriyyah, but I am asking.” She said: “This used to happen to us, and we were commanded to make up the fast but we were not commanded to make up the prayer.”(5) So the command to make up is a branch of the obligation of performance.

Consensus is established on preventing them both (the menstruating woman and the woman in postpartum bleeding) from fasting and on the obligation of making up upon them, as will come.(6)

(1) See: “Al-Bada'i'” (2/600), “Majma‘ al-Anhur” (1/373), “Al-Fatawa al-Hindiyyah” (1/214), “Al-Mughni” (4/218), “Kashshaf al-Qina‘” (2/308), “Al-Ifsah” (1/381), “Al-Majmu‘” (7/415), and “Al-Sharh al-Saghir” (1/440). Also: “Maraqi al-Falah” (p. 348).

(2) Al-Baqarah 2:185.

(3) “Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah” (p. 78).

(4) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (304).

(5) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (231) and Muslim (335).

(6) “Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah” (p. 77) and “Mughni al-Muhtaj” (1/432).
