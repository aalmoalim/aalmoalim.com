Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Inshiqaq (The Splitting Open)

Introduction: Surah Al-Inshiqaq is the third surah in the series that the Prophet (peace be upon him) mentioned: "Whoever wishes to look at the Day of Judgment as if seeing it with their own eyes, let them read: 'When the sun is wrapped up' (At-Takwir), 'When the sky is split open' (Al-Infitar), and 'When the sky is cleft asunder' (Al-Inshiqaq)." This surah completes the vivid picture of the Day of Judgment and emphasizes the concept of human toil (kadḥ) - that every person works and strives, and will meet the result of their work.

The Opening: The Sky Splits Open

إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انشَقَّتْ
"When the sky has split [open]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:1]

The surah opens with the sky splitting open completely. The word انشَقَّتْ (inshaqqat) means to split or cleave with severity and without return. This is different from انفَطَرَتْ (infatarat) in Al-Infitar, which was the first crack. Here, the splitting is complete and severe.

وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ
"And has responded to its Lord and was obligated [to do so]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:2]

The sky did not split of its own accord. It split in response to its Lord's command. The word أَذِنَتْ (adhinat) means it listened attentively, waiting for its Lord's command. The word حُقَّتْ (ḥuqqat) means the command fell upon it, and it was obligated to respond - it had no choice but to obey.

Key Contrast: The surah shows a contrast between the creation and the disbelievers:

  • The sky and earth: Listen attentively to their Lord's command and obey
  • The disbelievers: When the Quran is recited to them, they don't listen, don't prostrate, and reject

Even the vast heavens, despite their greatness, submit to Allah's command, while humans who are weak and created from a drop of fluid refuse to submit.

The Earth Stretches Out

وَإِذَا الْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ
"And when the earth has been extended"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:3]
وَأَلْقَتْ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتْ
"And has cast out that within it and relinquished [it]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:4]
وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ
"And has responded to its Lord and was obligated [to do so]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:5]

The earth will be stretched out (either flattened or extended), cast out everything within it (the dead, treasures), and relinquish all responsibility. The word تَخَلَّتْ (takhallat) means it wants to be free from any burden or accountability. It doesn't want to be asked about anything.

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "The earth will vomit out pieces of its liver like columns of gold and silver on the Day of Judgment. The murderer will pass by and say: 'For this I killed.' The thief will pass by and say: 'For this I stole.' The one who cut family ties will pass by and say: 'For this I cut my family ties.' Then they will leave it and go."

All the treasures of the world will be exposed, but no one will care about them.

O Mankind, You Are Toiling

يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلَاقِيهِ
"O mankind, indeed you are laboring toward your Lord with [great] exertion and will meet it"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:6]

This is the central message of the surah. Allah addresses all of humanity: "O mankind, you are toiling (kādiḥ) toward your Lord, and you will meet Him."

The Meaning of "Kadḥ" (Toil):

  • It means to work hard, to strive, to exert effort
  • It's less than a blow (kadmah) - it's like scratching or digging, searching for something
  • Everyone is searching for something: money, happiness, children, position
  • Everyone is working, everyone is tired, everyone is striving

The truth that people are unaware of: You are toiling toward your Lord - the end of this path is meeting Allah. You will meet the result of your work.

Allah says: "We have certainly created man into hardship" (Al-Balad 90:4). Everyone experiences hardship. The believer works hard, the disbeliever works hard. Everyone tastes exhaustion, everyone tastes toil.

Critical Point: The difference between the believer and disbeliever is not that one works and the other doesn't. Both work hard. The difference is:

  • The believer: Hopes from Allah what the disbelievers don't hope for
  • The disbeliever: Works hard but has no hope in the Hereafter

Allah says: "And if you are harmed, they are harmed as you are harmed. But you hope from Allah what they do not hope for" (An-Nisa 4:104).

The Result of Toil

فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ بِيَمِينِهِ
"So as for he who is given his record in his right hand"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:7]

After the toil comes the result. The one who receives their record in their right hand - receiving with the right hand indicates strength, honor, and pride. They come forward to receive it, happy with what they worked for.

فَسَوْفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابًا يَسِيرًا
"He will be judged with an easy account"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:8]

They will be judged with an easy account - just a quick review. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that Allah will bring the believer close, cover them, and remind them of their sins. The believer will acknowledge them, and Allah will say: "I covered them for you in the world, and today I forgive them for you." This is the easy account - just a reminder of Allah's blessings.

وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰ أَهْلِهِ مَسْرُورًا
"And return to his people, rejoicing"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:9]

They will return to their family, rejoicing. They were careful with their family in this world - warning them, encouraging them, waking them for prayer. Now they return to them happy, saying: "Didn't I tell you that a day would come when we would rejoice together? Didn't I tell you that we would meet the reward of this toil?"

وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ وَرَاءَ ظَهْرِهِ
"But as for he who is given his record behind his back"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:10]

Notice that it doesn't say "in his left hand" but "behind his back." Scholars explain that their right hand will be tied to their neck, and their left hand will be placed behind their back, so they take the record from behind their back.

Why Behind the Back?

  • Allah doesn't want to see them - the One giving them the record doesn't want to look at them
  • They cannot face their Lord out of shame and embarrassment
  • They were used to doing evil deeds secretly, behind people's backs - so they receive their record from behind their back
فَسَوْفَ يَدْعُو ثُبُورًا
"He will cry out for destruction"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:11]
وَيَصْلَىٰ سَعِيرًا
"And [enter to] burn in a Blaze"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:12]

They will call for destruction, saying: "O my destruction! O my regret!" But it won't help them. Then they will burn in Hellfire. They chose the path of desires, and the path of desires ends in Hellfire.

إِنَّهُ كَانَ فِي أَهْلِهِ مَسْرُورًا
"Indeed, he had been among his people, happy"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:13]

They were happy among their family - either because they were heedless and didn't think about the Hereafter, or because they were happy at the harm they caused to the believers (as mentioned in Al-Mutaffifin: "And when they returned to their people, they would return jesting").

إِنَّهُ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ
"Indeed, he had thought he would never return [to Allah]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:14]

They thought they would never return - either that they wouldn't be resurrected, or that conditions would remain as they were (they would stay powerful, rich, and arrogant). But Allah says: "Lowering and raising" - conditions will change. The one who was raised in this world may be lowered in the Hereafter, and vice versa.

The Oath: Conditions Will Change

كَلَّا ۖ بَل رَّبُّكَ يَرَاقِبُ
"No! But indeed, your Lord is ever watchful"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:15]
فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِالشَّفَقِ
"So I swear by the twilight glow"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:16]
وَاللَّيْلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ
"And [by] the night and what it envelops"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:17]
وَالْقَمَرِ إِذَا اتَّسَقَ
"And [by] the moon when it becomes full"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:18]

Allah swears by the twilight (the red glow mixed with darkness at sunset), the night and what it contains, and the full moon. These represent the natural progression: twilight (fear and uncertainty), then complete darkness (night), then light appears (full moon).

لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍ
"You will surely experience state after state"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:19]

You will surely experience state after state - you will move from one condition to another. This can mean:

  • For the disbelievers: You will move from this world to the grave, then to the Day of Judgment, then to Hellfire - each state worse than the previous
  • For the Prophet (peace be upon him): You will move from Makkah to Madinah, then the world, and ascend to the heavens (Mi'raj)
  • General meaning: Conditions will definitely change - this is Allah's sunnah (way) in creation

The Promise of Change: This is a fundamental principle - conditions will not remain as they are. The oppressed will not remain oppressed forever. The oppressors will not remain in power forever. This is Allah's sunnah in the universe.

The Rejection of the Disbelievers

فَمَا لَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ
"So what is [the matter] with them [that] they do not believe"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:20]
وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْقُرْآنُ لَا يَسْجُدُونَ ۩
"And when the Qur'an is recited to them, they do not prostrate [to Allah]"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:21]

What's wrong with them that they don't believe? When the Quran is recited to them, they don't prostrate. The sky listens to its Lord's command and obeys, but these people don't listen to Allah's word and don't prostrate.

بَلِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا يُكَذِّبُونَ
"But those who have disbelieved deny"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:22]
وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ
"And Allah is most knowing of what they keep within themselves"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:23]

They don't just not listen - they actively deny. And Allah knows what they conceal in their chests - envy, hatred, desires, plots against the believers.

فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ
"So give them tidings of a painful punishment"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:24]
إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ
"Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds. For them is a reward uninterrupted"
[Al-Inshiqaq 84:25]

Give them tidings of painful punishment - except those who believe and do righteous deeds. For them is a reward that is not cut off. Their toil will not be wasted. They will be rewarded with Paradise, by Allah's permission.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Everyone Works Hard

Everyone toils and works hard - believers and disbelievers alike. The difference is in what they hope for and where their toil leads.

2. The End of All Toil

All toil leads to meeting Allah. You will meet the result of your work. Make sure your toil is for Allah's sake so it's not wasted.

3. Conditions Will Change

This is Allah's sunnah - conditions will not remain as they are. The oppressed will be raised, and the oppressors will be lowered.

4. The Contrast

The creation (sky and earth) obeys Allah's command, while humans who were given free will often refuse to obey.

5. The Two Outcomes

Those who receive their record in their right hand will have an easy account and return to their family rejoicing. Those who receive it behind their back will call for destruction and burn in Hellfire.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Inshiqaq completes the vivid picture of the Day of Judgment started in At-Takwir and Al-Infitar. It emphasizes that:

  • Everyone works hard and toils
  • All toil leads to meeting Allah
  • Conditions will definitely change
  • The creation obeys Allah while many humans refuse
  • There are two clear outcomes based on one's deeds

The surah calls people to realize that their toil should be for Allah's sake, so it's not wasted, and they can return to their families rejoicing on the Day of Judgment.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) khutbah (sermon) of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Inshiqaq.
