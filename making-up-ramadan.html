Making Up Ramadan

Whoever breaks his fast for days from Ramadan, like the sick person and the traveler and the like, makes up after it what he missed, because making up must be after it, due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"So whoever among you is ill or on a journey, then [he should fast] a number of other days"
[Al-Baqarah 2:185]

And whoever missed the fast of Ramadan completely, whether Ramadan was complete, or incomplete, he makes up the number of its days, whether he began it from the beginning of the month or from its middle, like the numbers of missed prayers, because making up must be by the number of what was missed, due to His statement, the Exalted: "a number of other days."

And it is permissible to make up a day of winter for a day of summer, and the opposite, by making up a day of summer for a day of winter, due to the generality of the verse.(1)

(1) "Kashaf al-Qina'" (2/333), "Matlab Uli al-Nuha" (2/208), "Al-Insaf" (3/333), and "Jawahir al-Iklil" (1/153, 154).
