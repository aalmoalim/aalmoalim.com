Generosity and Good Deeds in Ramadan

Generosity is recommended at all times, and it is more emphasized in Ramadan, and in the last ten days it is best, following the example of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, and the predecessors. And because it is a noble month, so a good deed in it is better than in other times. And because people are occupied in it with their fasting and increasing their acts of obedience, away from earning, so they need assistance and help.(1)

In the two Sahihs from Ibn 'Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them, who said:

"The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, was the most generous of people, and he was at his most generous in Ramadan when Jibril would meet him. He would meet him every night of Ramadan and study the Quran with him. So the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, was more generous with good than the sent wind."(2)

That is: in speed and generality.

(1) Al-Nawawi said this in "Al-Majmu'" (7/630).

(2) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1902, 322, 3554) and Muslim (2308).
