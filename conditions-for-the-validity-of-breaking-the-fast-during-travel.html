Conditions for the Validity of Breaking the Fast During Travel

The following are required in travel that permits breaking the fast:

a - That the travel be long enough to shorten prayer.

b - That the traveler not intend to reside during his travel for a period of four days and nights (according to the Malikis and Shafi'is), more than four days (according to the Hanbalis), or half a month or fifteen days (according to the Hanafis).

c - That his travel not be for disobedience.

d - That he pass beyond the city and what is connected to it, buildings, courtyards, and tents.
