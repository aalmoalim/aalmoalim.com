Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Qadr (The Power)

Introduction: Surah Al-Qadr is one of the most important surahs in the Quran, describing Laylat al-Qadr (The Night of Power) - the night when the Quran was first revealed to the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). This night is better than a thousand months, and it occurs in the last ten nights of Ramadan. The surah emphasizes the immense value and blessing of this night, when angels descend and peace prevails until dawn.

Special Status: The Prophet (peace be upon him) said:

  • Whoever prays on Laylat al-Qadr with faith and seeking reward, all his previous sins will be forgiven
  • This night is better than a thousand months
  • It occurs in the last ten nights of Ramadan, particularly on the odd nights (21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th, or 29th)
  • Angels descend on this night, bringing peace and blessings

The Revelation of the Quran

إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ
"Indeed, We sent the Quran down during the Night of Decree"
[Al-Qadr 97:1]

Indeed, We sent the Quran down during the Night of Decree (لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ - Laylat al-Qadr). The word "Qadr" means:

  • Decree/Destiny: On this night, Allah decrees all matters for the coming year
  • Honor/Value: This night has immense honor and value
  • Constrained: The night is constrained and limited - it is one specific night

This was the night when the Quran was first revealed to the Prophet (peace be upon him) in the Cave of Hira, beginning with the first verses of Surah Al-Alaq.

The Value: Better Than a Thousand Months

وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ
"And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree?"
[Al-Qadr 97:2]
لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ
"The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months"
[Al-Qadr 97:3]

The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. This means:

  • Worship on this night is better than worship for a thousand months (over 83 years)
  • One night of worship equals more than a lifetime of worship
  • This shows the immense value and blessing of this night

The Calculation: A thousand months equals approximately 83 years and 4 months. This means that worship on Laylat al-Qadr is equivalent to worshiping for more than an entire lifetime!

The Descent of Angels

تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ
"The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter"
[Al-Qadr 97:4]

The angels and the Spirit (Jibril) descend on this night by permission of their Lord for every matter. This means:

  • Angels descend in great numbers, filling the earth
  • They bring peace, blessings, and mercy
  • They record the decrees for the coming year
  • The presence of angels brings tranquility and peace

Peace Until Dawn

سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ
"Peace it is until the emergence of dawn"
[Al-Qadr 97:5]

Peace it is until the emergence of dawn. This night is filled with:

  • Peace from Allah: Divine peace and tranquility
  • Peace from angels: The angels greet believers with peace
  • Peace from evil: Satan cannot harm on this night
  • Complete peace: From sunset until dawn, the entire night is peaceful

Seeking Laylat al-Qadr: The Prophet (peace be upon him) said to seek it in the last ten nights of Ramadan, particularly on the odd nights. He also said that whoever misses it has missed much good.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Value of Time

This surah teaches us that one night of sincere worship can be worth more than a lifetime. Time is not just about quantity - quality and timing matter immensely.

2. The Blessing of Revelation

The Quran was revealed on this night, showing its importance and the blessing it brings to humanity.

3. The Presence of Angels

Angels descend on this night, bringing peace and blessings. Their presence is a sign of Allah's mercy and favor.

4. Seeking This Night

Believers should actively seek this night in the last ten nights of Ramadan, increasing their worship and supplication.

5. Peace and Tranquility

This night brings complete peace - from Allah, from angels, and protection from evil. It is a night of tranquility and blessing.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Qadr emphasizes that:

  • Laylat al-Qadr is the night when the Quran was revealed
  • This night is better than a thousand months
  • Angels descend on this night, bringing peace and blessings
  • The entire night is filled with peace until dawn
  • Believers should seek this night in the last ten nights of Ramadan
  • Worship on this night can erase previous sins and bring immense reward

The surah calls believers to recognize the immense value of this night and to actively seek it through increased worship, prayer, and supplication in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on standard tafsir (exegesis) of Surah Al-Qadr, drawing from classical and contemporary interpretations of the Quran.
