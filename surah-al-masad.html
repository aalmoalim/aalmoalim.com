Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Masad (The Palm Fiber)

Introduction: Surah Al-Masad (also known as Surah Al-Lahab) is one of the most powerful surahs in the Quran. It was revealed about Abu Lahab, the uncle of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), who was one of his fiercest enemies. This surah appears near the end of the Quran, before the Mu'awwidhat (Al-Falaq and An-Nas), serving as a reminder of the beginning of the call to Islam and how Allah supports His Prophet. The surah declares that Abu Lahab and his wife are doomed to Hellfire, and that their wealth and efforts will not save them.

The Opening: The Curse on Abu Lahab

تَبَّتْ يَدَا أَبِي لَهَبٍ وَتَبَّ
"May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined, and ruined is he"
[Al-Masad 111:1]

May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined, and ruined is he. The word تَبَّتْ (tabbat) means to be cut off and destroyed. This is a declaration that Abu Lahab's hands (his power and strength) are destroyed, and he himself is destroyed. This was revealed when Abu Lahab cursed the Prophet (peace be upon him), and the curse was returned upon him.

مَا أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُ مَالُهُ وَمَا كَسَبَ
"His wealth will not avail him or that which he gained"
[Al-Masad 111:2]

His wealth will not avail him or that which he gained. All the wealth Abu Lahab accumulated and all his efforts to oppose the Prophet (peace be upon him) will not help him. He will face punishment despite his wealth and power.

The Reality:

  • Abu Lahab's wealth and power will not save him
  • All his efforts to oppose the Prophet will be in vain
  • He will face punishment in Hellfire
  • His legacy is only remembered with curses

The Punishment: The Blazing Fire

سَيَصْلَىٰ نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ
"He will [enter to] burn in a Fire of [blazing] flame"
[Al-Masad 111:3]

He will burn in a Fire of blazing flame. The word "lahab" (flame) is significant - Abu Lahab's name means "father of flame," and he will burn in the Fire of Hell. His name itself points to his fate.

His Wife: The Carrier of Firewood

وَامْرَأَتُهُ حَمَّالَةَ الْحَطَبِ
"And his wife [as well] - the carrier of firewood"
[Al-Masad 111:4]

And his wife [as well] - the carrier of firewood. Abu Lahab's wife, Umm Jamil, was known for carrying thorns and placing them in the path of the Prophet (peace be upon him). She also spread slander and gossip. In Hellfire, she will carry firewood to fuel the Fire that burns her and her husband.

فِي جِيدِهَا حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ
"Around her neck is a rope of [twisted] fiber"
[Al-Masad 111:5]

Around her neck is a rope of twisted fiber. In Hellfire, she will have a rope of palm fiber around her neck, and she will carry firewood to fuel the Fire. This is a fitting punishment for someone who spread slander and placed obstacles in the path of the Prophet (peace be upon him).

The Punishment Matches the Crime:

  • She placed thorns in the Prophet's path → She will carry firewood in Hellfire
  • She spread slander and gossip → She will have a rope around her neck
  • She opposed the Prophet with her husband → They will both burn together

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Allah Supports His Prophet

When Abu Lahab cursed the Prophet (peace be upon him), Allah responded by revealing this surah, declaring that Abu Lahab and his wife are doomed to Hellfire.

2. Wealth and Power Cannot Save

Abu Lahab's wealth and power did not save him. Those who oppose Allah and His Prophet will face punishment regardless of their worldly status.

3. The Punishment is Certain

This surah was revealed as a prophecy - Abu Lahab died as a disbeliever, and he and his wife will burn in Hellfire. The Quran's prediction came true.

4. Spouses Can Influence Each Other

Abu Lahab's wife supported him in opposing the Prophet (peace be upon him), and she will share in his punishment. This shows the importance of choosing righteous spouses.

5. The Beginning and End of the Call

This surah, appearing near the end of the Quran, reminds us of the beginning of the call to Islam and how Allah supported His Prophet from the start.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Masad emphasizes that:

  • Abu Lahab and his wife are doomed to Hellfire
  • Their wealth and efforts will not save them
  • Allah supports His Prophet against his enemies
  • The punishment matches the crime
  • Those who oppose Allah and His Prophet will face certain punishment

The surah serves as a powerful reminder that Allah defends His Prophet (peace be upon him) and that those who oppose him will face severe punishment, regardless of their worldly status or power.
