Reward for Those Who Break Their Fast During the Day of Ramadan

From Abu Umamah al-Bahili, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: I heard the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, say:

"While I was sleeping, two men came to me and took hold of my arms, and they brought me to a rugged mountain. They said to me: 'Climb up.' I said: 'I cannot do it.' They said: 'We will make it easy for you.' So I climbed until when I was at the middle of the mountain, I heard loud voices. I said: 'What are these voices?' It was said: 'This is the howling of the people of the Fire.' Then they took me, and I saw people suspended by their heels, their mouths split, and their mouths flowing with blood. I said: 'Who are these?' It was said: 'These are those who break their fast before the time for breaking their fast is due.'"(1)

Meaning: those who break their fast before the time of breaking the fast.

(1) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Ibn Khuzaymah in his "Sahih" (3/237), Ibn Hibban in his "Sahih" (16/536), Al-Nasa'i in "Al-Kubra" (2/246), and Al-Hakim in "Al-Mustadrak" (1/595, 228).
