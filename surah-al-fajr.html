Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Fajr (The Dawn)

Introduction: Surah Al-Fajr is a Makkan surah that swears by blessed times and then describes the destruction of powerful oppressors. The main theme is that believers should find tranquility through worship and obedience to Allah, not through material means. Those who rely on material means (pillars, fortresses, armies) will be destroyed, while those who rely on Allah and worship Him will find peace.

The Opening Oaths: Blessed Times

وَالْفَجْرِ
"By the dawn"
[Al-Fajr 89:1]
وَلَيَالٍ عَشْرٍ
"And [by] ten nights"
[Al-Fajr 89:2]
وَالشَّفْعِ وَالْوَتْرِ
"And [by] the even [number] and the odd"
[Al-Fajr 89:3]
وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا يَسْرِ
"And [by] the night when it passes"
[Al-Fajr 89:4]

Allah swears by blessed times. Scholars differ on the specific meanings:

  • Al-Fajr (The Dawn): Either the dawn of any day, or specifically the dawn of the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah (Day of Sacrifice), or the dawn of the 1st of Muharram
  • Ten Nights: The first ten nights of Dhul-Hijjah (most common), or the last ten nights of Ramadan, or the first ten nights of Muharram
  • Ash-Shaf' and Al-Watr (Even and Odd): All types of worship (even and odd), or the Day of Arafah (9th - odd) and Day of Sacrifice (10th - even), or all creation (even pairs) and Allah (the One - odd)
  • Al-Layl idhā yasr (The Night When It Passes): The night when the believer travels through it in worship

The Main Purpose: These blessed times are:

  1. Times of worship: The believer eagerly awaits these times to worship Allah
  2. Times of destruction: Times when Allah destroyed oppressors (some scholars say)
  3. Times of alternation: Showing Allah's ability to alternate between day and night, and between victory and defeat

The key message: The believer finds tranquility through worship, not through material means.

هَلْ فِي ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمٌ لِّذِي حِجْرٍ
"Is there [not] in [all] that an oath [sufficient] for one of perception?"
[Al-Fajr 89:5]

Is there not in that an oath for one of perception? The word حِجْر (ḥijr) means intellect or restraint - the intellect that restrains you from sin. The one with intellect will reflect on these blessed times and use them for worship.

The Destruction of Oppressors

أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ
"Have you not considered how your Lord dealt with 'Aad?"
[Al-Fajr 89:6]
إِرَمَ ذَاتِ الْعِمَادِ
"[With] Iram - who had lofty pillars"
[Al-Fajr 89:7]
الَّتِي لَمْ يُخْلَقْ مِثْلُهَا فِي الْبِلَادِ
"The likes of whom had never been created in the land"
[Al-Fajr 89:8]

Allah destroyed 'Aad - Iram with lofty pillars. They had reached the peak of control over material means - either through their physical strength (tall bodies) or through their buildings and fortresses. They thought they were invincible.

وَثَمُودَ الَّذِينَ جَابُوا الصَّخْرَ بِالْوَادِي
"And [with] Thamud, who cut out the rocks in the valley"
[Al-Fajr 89:9]

And Thamud, who cut out the rocks in the valley - they carved homes in the mountains, thinking they were safe from destruction. They fortified themselves in stone, but Allah destroyed them with a sound (ṣayḥah).

وَفِرْعَوْنَ ذِي الْأَوْتَادِ
"And [with] Pharaoh, owner of the stakes"
[Al-Fajr 89:10]

And Pharaoh, owner of the stakes - either his armies (that secured his kingdom), the pyramids (lofty buildings), or the stakes he used to torture those who opposed him. He relied on these material means.

The Pattern: All three groups relied on material means:

  • 'Aad: Lofty pillars (buildings/strength)
  • Thamud: Rock fortresses in the valley
  • Pharaoh: Stakes (armies/buildings/torture tools)

All of these means were useless when Allah's punishment came.

الَّذِينَ طَغَوْا فِي الْبِلَادِ
"Who were arrogant within the lands"
[Al-Fajr 89:11]
فَأَكْثَرُوا فِيهَا الْفَسَادَ
"And increased therein the corruption"
[Al-Fajr 89:12]

They were arrogant in the lands - they transgressed boundaries and became tyrants. They increased corruption - wherever there are no boundaries and no rules, corruption spreads.

The Chain: Control over means → Feeling of self-sufficiency → Arrogance → Transgression → Corruption → Destruction

The believer breaks this chain by never feeling self-sufficient, always recognizing dependence on Allah, even when they have means.

فَصَبَّ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّكَ سَوْطَ عَذَابٍ
"So your Lord poured upon them a scourge of punishment"
[Al-Fajr 89:13]

So your Lord poured upon them a scourge of punishment. The word سَوْط (sawṭ) means a whip, and here it indicates:

  • Pain: Like a whip strike - painful
  • Speed: Like a whip strike - fast and sudden
  • Mixture: A combination of different types of punishment
إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِالْمِرْصَادِ
"Indeed, your Lord is in observation"
[Al-Fajr 89:14]

Indeed, your Lord is in observation - like a lion watching its prey, waiting for the right moment. Allah watches the oppressors, and when the time comes, He strikes. Don't think Allah is unaware - He is watching and waiting.

The Wrong Understanding of Blessings

فَأَمَّا الْإِنسَانُ إِذَا مَا ابْتَلَاهُ رَبُّهُ فَأَكْرَمَهُ وَنَعَّمَهُ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّي أَكْرَمَنِ
"And as for man, when his Lord tries him and [thus] is generous to him and favors him, he says, 'My Lord has honored me'"
[Al-Fajr 89:15]
وَأَمَّا إِذَا مَا ابْتَلَاهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقَهُ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّي أَهَانَنِ
"But when He tries him and restricts his provision, he says, 'My Lord has humiliated me'"
[Al-Fajr 89:16]
كَلَّا ۖ بَل لَّا تُكْرِمُونَ الْيَتِيمَ
"No! But you do not honor the orphan"
[Al-Fajr 89:17]

People mistakenly interpret wealth as honor and poverty as humiliation. But Allah says: "No!" - this is wrong thinking. The real honor is being guided to obedience, and the real humiliation is being left to disobedience.

The Materialistic Interpretation: Many people interpret everything in material terms:

  • Wealth = Allah loves me
  • Poverty = Allah doesn't love me
  • Sickness = Allah is angry
  • Health = Allah is pleased

This is wrong. Allah gives this world to both those He loves and those He doesn't love. The real measure is guidance to obedience.

وَلَا تَحَاضُّونَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ
"And you do not encourage one another to feed the poor"
[Al-Fajr 89:18]
وَتَأْكُلُونَ التُّرَاثَ أَكْلًا لَّمًّا
"And you consume inheritance, devouring [it] altogether"
[Al-Fajr 89:19]
وَتُحِبُّونَ الْمَالَ حُبًّا جَمًّا
"And you love wealth with immense love"
[Al-Fajr 89:20]

You don't honor the orphan, you don't encourage feeding the poor, you devour inheritance (taking what doesn't belong to you), and you love wealth immensely. This is the behavior of those who interpret everything materially.

The Day of Judgment

كَلَّا ۖ إِذَا دُكَّتِ الْأَرْضُ دَكًّا دَكًّا
"No! When the earth has been leveled - pounded and crushed"
[Al-Fajr 89:21]
وَجَاءَ رَبُّكَ وَالْمَلَكُ صَفًّا صَفًّا
"And your Lord comes and the angels, rank upon rank"
[Al-Fajr 89:22]
وَجِيءَ يَوْمَئِذٍ بِجَهَنَّمَ ۚ يَوْمَئِذٍ يَتَذَكَّرُ الْإِنسَانُ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُ الذِّكْرَىٰ
"And brought that Day, is Hell - that Day, man will remember, but what good to him will be the remembrance?"
[Al-Fajr 89:23]

When the earth is leveled, and your Lord comes with the angels in ranks, and Hell is brought forth - then man will remember. But what good will remembrance do then? It's too late.

يَقُولُ يَا لَيْتَنِي قَدَّمْتُ لِحَيَاتِي
"He will say, 'Oh, I wish I had sent ahead [some good] for my life'"
[Al-Fajr 89:24]

He will say: "Oh, I wish I had sent ahead good deeds for my life" - meaning for my real life, which is the Hereafter. This world is not the real life - the Hereafter is the real life.

فَيَوْمَئِذٍ لَّا يُعَذِّبُ عَذَابَهُ أَحَدٌ
"So on that Day, none will punish [as severely] as His punishment"
[Al-Fajr 89:25]
وَلَا يُوثِقُ وَثَاقَهُ أَحَدٌ
"And none will bind [as severely] as His binding [of the evildoers]"
[Al-Fajr 89:26]

On that Day, none will punish as severely as Allah's punishment, and none will bind as severely as Allah's binding. The punishment of the oppressors in this world is nothing compared to what awaits them.

The Call to the Contented Soul

يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ
"O reassured soul"
[Al-Fajr 89:27]
ارْجِعِي إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً
"Return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing [to Him]"
[Al-Fajr 89:28]
فَادْخُلِي فِي عِبَادِي
"And enter among My [righteous] servants"
[Al-Fajr 89:29]
وَادْخُلِي جَنَّتِي
"And enter My Paradise"
[Al-Fajr 89:30]

O reassured soul - the soul that found tranquility through worship, through prayer at dawn, through the ten nights, through various acts of worship, through traveling in the night in worship. This soul did not find tranquility through material means (pillars, fortresses, armies) but through obedience to Allah.

The Contented Soul: This is the soul that:

  • Found tranquility through worship, not through material means
  • Used the blessed times (dawn, ten nights, night) for worship
  • Did not rely on pillars, fortresses, or armies
  • Trusted in Allah and feared His promise

This soul is called to return to Allah, well-pleased and pleasing to Him, and to enter Paradise with the righteous servants.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Blessed Times for Worship

Allah swears by blessed times (dawn, ten nights, even and odd, night) - these are times the believer eagerly awaits to worship Allah. These times provide spiritual nourishment throughout the year.

2. Material Means Are Useless

Those who rely on material means (pillars, fortresses, armies) will be destroyed. Material means don't provide real security - only Allah does.

3. The Chain of Destruction

Control over means → Self-sufficiency → Arrogance → Transgression → Corruption → Destruction. The believer breaks this chain by always recognizing dependence on Allah.

4. Wrong Interpretation of Blessings

Wealth doesn't mean Allah loves you, and poverty doesn't mean Allah doesn't love you. The real measure is guidance to obedience.

5. The Contented Soul

The soul that finds tranquility through worship, not through material means, is the one that will be called to Paradise.

6. Allah is Watching

Allah is in observation - He watches the oppressors and will punish them at the right time. Don't be impatient.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Fajr emphasizes that:

  • Believers should find tranquility through worship in blessed times, not through material means
  • Those who rely on material means will be destroyed, as shown by the examples of 'Aad, Thamud, and Pharaoh
  • Material blessings don't indicate Allah's love - guidance to obedience does
  • The contented soul is the one that relied on Allah and worship, not on material means
  • Allah is watching and will hold everyone accountable

The surah calls believers to use the blessed times for worship and to find tranquility through obedience to Allah, not through the material means that the oppressors relied on.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Fajr.
