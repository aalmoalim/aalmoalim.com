Consecutiveness in Making Up Ramadan

The four imams have agreed that making up the month of Ramadan is valid when done separately, and that consecutiveness is better. That is due to the generality of the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"a number of other days"
[Al-Baqarah 2:185]

And because it is a fast that is not attached to a specific time, so consecutiveness is not required in it, like an absolute vow.

And consecutiveness is better, because in it is hastening to the performance of the obligation, and hastening to drop the obligation.(1)

(1) "Al-Ifsah" (1/416), "Al-Mudawwanah" (1/188), "Sharh Fath al-Qadir" (2/353, 354), "Al-'Inayah" (2/354), "Bulghat al-Salik" (1/691), "Al-Majmu'" (7/616), and "Al-Mughni" (4/214).
