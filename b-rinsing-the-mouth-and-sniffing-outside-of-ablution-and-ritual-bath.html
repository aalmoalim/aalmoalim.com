Rinsing the Mouth and Sniffing Outside of Ablution and Ritual Bath

Rinsing the mouth and sniffing outside of ablution and ritual bath is not disliked, and it does not break the fast, due to what was narrated from the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, that 'Umar ibn al-Khattab, may Allah be pleased with him, asked him about kissing for the fasting person. So he, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "What do you think if you rinsed your mouth with water while fasting?" I said: There is no harm in it. He said: "Then why?"(1) And because the mouth is in the ruling of the exterior, so fasting is not invalidated by what reaches it, like the nose and the eye.

However, the Malikis restricted it to if it is for thirst and the like, and they disliked it for other than a cause, because in it is risk and danger, and that is due to the possibility of something from the water preceding to the throat, so it invalidates fasting then.(2)

Ibn Qudamah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: As for rinsing the mouth for other than purification, if it is for a need, like washing one's mouth when there is a need for it and the like, its ruling is the ruling of rinsing for purification. And if one is playing around, or one rinses for the sake of thirst, it is disliked. Ahmad was asked about the fasting person who becomes thirsty, so he rinses then expels it. He said: Sprinkling on one's chest is more beloved to me.(3)

There is no harm in pouring water on one's head from heat and thirst, due to what was narrated from some of the companions of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, that he said:

"I indeed saw the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, at al-'Arj pouring water on his head from thirst or from heat."(4)

The Hanafis said: Rinsing the mouth, sniffing, ritual bath, and wrapping oneself in a wet garment for cooling and repelling heat are not disliked according to what is issued fatwa on according to them, and it is the view of Abu Yusuf, may Allah have mercy on him, due to the previously mentioned hadith.

Ibn 'Abidin, may Allah have mercy on him, said: And because these things contain help for him in worship and repelling natural boredom. Abu Hanifah disliked them due to what is in them of showing boredom in worship.(5)

(1) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Abu Dawud (2385).

(2) "Al-Sharh al-Kabir wa-Hashiyat al-Dusuqi" (1/534), "Sharh Mukhtasar Khalil" (2/260), "Manh al-Jalil" (2/148), and "Al-Sharh al-Saghir" (1/463).

(3) "Al-Mughni" (4/165).

(4) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Abu Dawud (2365) and Ahmad (5/376).

(5) "Hashiyat Ibn 'Abidin" (2/419).
