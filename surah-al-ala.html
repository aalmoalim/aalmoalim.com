Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-A'la (The Most High)

Introduction: Surah Al-A'la is a Makkan surah that holds special significance. The Prophet (peace be upon him) used to recite it frequently - in the first rak'ah of Witr prayer, in Friday prayer, and in Eid prayer. This repetition indicates its importance. The surah comes after Al-Buruj (which showed the persecution of believers) and At-Tariq (which showed the plotting of the disbelievers), and it serves to exalt Allah and reassure believers that He is the Most High, above all deficiencies.

Special Status: The Prophet (peace be upon him) would recite this surah:

  • In the first rak'ah of Witr prayer
  • In Friday prayer (after the khutbah)
  • In Eid prayer

This frequent repetition shows the surah's importance in strengthening faith, especially during times of difficulty and oppression.

The Opening: Exalt the Name of Your Lord, the Most High

سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْأَعْلَى
"Exalt the name of your Lord, the Most High"
[Al-A'la 87:1]

The surah begins with a command to exalt (tasbīḥ) the name of Allah, the Most High. The word سَبِّح (sabbih) means to declare free from all deficiencies. After seeing the persecution in Al-Buruj and the plotting in At-Tariq, believers might feel despair or have doubts. This surah comes to purify Allah's name from any deficiency that might come to mind.

Critical Context: After witnessing:

  • The persecution and burning of believers (Al-Buruj)
  • The secret plotting and scheming of the disbelievers (At-Tariq)

Believers might think: "Why does Allah leave us? Why do the disbelievers have wealth and power while we suffer?"

This surah comes to say: Exalt the name of your Lord, the Most High - never think that Allah has any deficiency. He is above everything and everyone.

The word الْأَعْلَى (al-a'lā - "the Most High") is crucial here. It emphasizes that Allah is above all - above every disbeliever, above every oppressor. No matter how much power, wealth, or children the disbelievers have, Allah is above them all.

Allah's Attributes: The Creator

الَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ
"Who created and proportioned"
[Al-A'la 87:2]
وَالَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ
"And who destined and [then] guided"
[Al-A'la 87:3]

Allah is the One who created and proportioned - there is no deficiency in His creation. He destined and guided - He gave each creature its specific ability and guided it to discover and use it. The bird knows how to fly, the fish knows how to swim, the child knows how to grow - all by Allah's guidance.

Key Point: These attributes remind believers that Allah's actions are never without wisdom. Even when believers are being persecuted, there is wisdom in it. Allah says: "And if Allah had willed, He could have given them victory over you, but [He allows it] to test some of you with others" (Muhammad 47:4).

Two Types of Sustenance

وَالَّذِي أَخْرَجَ الْمَرْعَىٰ
"And who brings out the pasture"
[Al-A'la 87:4]
فَجَعَلَهُ غُثَاءً أَحْوَىٰ
"And [then] makes it [scattered] debris, blackened"
[Al-A'la 87:5]

Allah brings out the pasture - this is the material sustenance for the body. Both humans and animals share in this. But this sustenance has an end - it becomes blackened debris, scattered by the wind. This is the first type of sustenance - material, temporary, and perishable.

سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰ
"We will make you recite, [O Muhammad], and you will not forget"
[Al-A'la 87:6]
إِلَّا مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّهُ يَعْلَمُ الْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ
"Except what Allah should will. Indeed, He knows what is declared and what is hidden"
[Al-A'la 87:7]

Then comes the second type of sustenance - spiritual sustenance for the soul. The Prophet (peace be upon him) was concerned about preserving the revelation, so Allah reassures him: "We will make you recite, and you will not forget" - except what Allah wills to be abrogated.

The Journey of Revelation: This completes the reassurance about the preservation of revelation:

  1. Al-Buruj: "Indeed, it is a glorious Qur'an in a Preserved Slate" - preserved in the heavens
  2. At-Tariq: Protected from devils during revelation by the piercing stars
  3. Al-A'la: "We will make you recite, and you will not forget" - preserved in the Prophet's heart

As long as the Quran exists, the religion will continue, and new believers will emerge.

Facilitation for Righteous Deeds

وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ
"And We will ease you toward ease"
[Al-A'la 87:8]

Allah will facilitate the Prophet (peace be upon him) for ease - both the path will be made easy, and he will be made capable of ease. The path of calling to Allah, which people think is difficult, will be made easy for him.

Facilitation: Sometimes the path is easy, but the person finds it difficult. Sometimes the person is made capable, so even a difficult path becomes easy. Here, both happen - the path is facilitated, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) is made capable.

The Call to Remind

فَذَكِّرْ إِن نَّفَعَتِ الذِّكْرَىٰ
"So remind, if the reminder should benefit"
[Al-A'la 87:9]

So remind - continue calling people to Allah. Scholars differ on the meaning of "if the reminder should benefit":

  • Some say: If you think it will benefit, then remind
  • Others say: Remind regardless, but the benefit is for those who accept it

The key is to continue calling, but with wisdom - don't waste time with those who are obstinate and only want to argue, while neglecting those who are receptive.

سَيَذَّكَّرُ مَن يَخْشَىٰ
"He who fears [Allah] will be reminded"
[Al-A'la 87:10]
وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا الْأَشْقَى
"But the wretched one will avoid it"
[Al-A'la 87:11]

He who fears will be reminded - the one who has some fear in his heart, who questions what will happen after death, who feels that this universe couldn't have been created in vain. This person will benefit from the reminder.

The wretched one will avoid it - the one who chooses the pleasures of this world over the Hereafter.

The Two Outcomes

الَّذِي يَصْلَى النَّارَ الْكُبْرَىٰ
"Who will [enter and] burn in the greatest Fire"
[Al-A'la 87:12]
ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ
"Neither dying therein nor living"
[Al-A'la 87:13]

The wretched one will burn in the greatest Fire - not the small fire of this world, but the great Fire of the Hereafter. He will neither die (to rest) nor live (a normal life) - he will be in a state between life and death, the most severe form of torment.

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ
"He has certainly succeeded who purifies himself"
[Al-A'la 87:14]
وَذَكَرَ اسْمَ رَبِّهِ فَصَلَّىٰ
"And mentions the name of his Lord and prays"
[Al-A'la 87:15]

He has succeeded who purifies himself - who purifies himself from evil qualities and develops good qualities. The word تَزَكَّىٰ (tazakkā) means both purification and growth.

And mentions the name of his Lord and prays - when he remembers Allah, he longs for prayer. This is living remembrance - remembrance that leads to action.

The Obstacle: Preferring This World

بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا
"But you prefer the worldly life"
[Al-A'la 87:16]
وَالْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ
"While the Hereafter is better and more lasting"[Al-A'la 87:17]

But you prefer the worldly life - this is the main obstacle preventing people from purifying themselves and remembering Allah. When there's a conflict between this world and the Hereafter, they choose this world.

إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِي الصُّحُفِ الْأُولَىٰ
"Indeed, this is in the former scriptures"
[Al-A'la 87:18]
صُحُفِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ
"The scriptures of Abraham and Moses"
[Al-A'la 87:19]

Indeed, this is in the former scriptures - these teachings are not new. They were in the scriptures of Abraham and Moses. This is a constant need for all humanity - to turn to Allah, to purify oneself, and to prefer the Hereafter over this world.

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Exalt Allah Above All Deficiencies

Never think that Allah has any deficiency, no matter what you see in this world. He is the Most High, above everything and everyone.

2. Two Types of Sustenance

There is material sustenance (for the body) which perishes, and spiritual sustenance (the Quran, for the soul) which remains and never runs out.

3. The Revelation is Preserved

The Quran is preserved in the Preserved Slate, protected during revelation, and preserved in the Prophet's heart. As long as it exists, the religion will continue.

4. The Path is Made Easy

Allah facilitates the path of calling to Him and makes the caller capable of it.

5. Continue Calling

Continue reminding people, but with wisdom. Those who fear will benefit, while the wretched will avoid it.

6. Purification and Remembrance

Success comes through purifying oneself and remembering Allah, which leads to prayer and good deeds.

7. Prefer the Hereafter

The main obstacle is preferring this world. The Hereafter is better and more lasting.

Conclusion

Surah Al-A'la serves to:

  • Exalt Allah above all deficiencies, especially after seeing persecution and plotting
  • Reassure about the preservation of revelation
  • Distinguish between material sustenance (temporary) and spiritual sustenance (eternal)
  • Call believers to purify themselves and remember Allah
  • Warn against preferring this world over the Hereafter

The surah elevates the believer above the concerns of this world and reminds them that Allah is the Most High, and the Hereafter is better and more lasting.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lectures of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-A'la in two parts.
