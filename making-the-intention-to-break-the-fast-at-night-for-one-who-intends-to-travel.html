Making the Intention to Break the Fast at Night for One Who Intends to Travel

Imam Ibn 'Abd al-Barr, may Allah have mercy on him, said: They have agreed regarding one who intends to travel in Ramadan that it is not permissible for him to make the intention to break the fast at night, because the traveler does not become a traveler by intention. Rather, he becomes a traveler by setting out on his travel or by beginning his preparation. And intention in travel is not like intention in residence, because if a traveler intends residence, he becomes a resident immediately, because residence does not require action. And if a resident intends travel, he does not become a traveler until he begins his travel and goes out from the residence. Then it is permissible for him to shorten prayer and the rulings of the traveler, except if making his preparation for travel and his action in it is considered like travel and going out from residence, then it would be required that he not be required to do anything in his eating before departing.

And they have unanimously agreed that if he walks in his travel until the houses of the village and city disappear, then he stops and eats, then an obstacle prevents him from setting out on that travel, expiation is not required upon him.(1)

(1) "Al-Istidhkar" (3/308).
