Everything You Need to Know About Surah Al-Ma'un (The Small Kindnesses)

Introduction: Surah Al-Ma'un addresses a critical issue: the separation between worship and daily life. The surah shows that those who deny the Day of Judgment treat others harshly (pushing away orphans, not feeding the poor), and even those who pray can be among the worst if they are heedless of their prayers and refuse to help others. This surah warns against the dangerous duality of performing religious rituals while neglecting the rights of others.

Part One: Denying the Day of Judgment

أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ
"Have you seen the one who denies the Recompense?"
[Al-Ma'un 107:1]

Have you seen the one who denies the Recompense? (الدِّينِ - ad-deen, meaning the Day of Judgment). This question asks us to identify those who deny accountability in the Hereafter.

فَذَٰلِكَ الَّذِي يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ
"For that is the one who drives away the orphan"
[Al-Ma'un 107:2]

For that is the one who drives away the orphan. The word يَدُعُّ (yadu'u) means to push away harshly, like pushing cattle. Those who deny the Day of Judgment treat orphans with contempt, pushing them away without mercy.

وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ
"And does not encourage the feeding of the poor"
[Al-Ma'un 107:3]

And does not encourage the feeding of the poor. They don't even encourage others to feed the poor - they are so far removed from mercy and compassion that they don't even promote basic kindness.

Signs of Denying the Day of Judgment:

  • Harsh treatment of orphans - pushing them away like cattle
  • Not feeding the poor or encouraging others to do so
  • Lack of compassion and mercy
  • Treating the weak with contempt

Part Two: The Hypocritical Worshippers

فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ
"So woe to those who pray"
[Al-Ma'un 107:4]

So woe to those who pray. This is shocking - woe to those who pray? Yes, because they pray but are heedless of what prayer means and requires.

الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ
"[But] who are heedless of their prayer"
[Al-Ma'un 107:5]

Who are heedless of their prayer. They pray but are heedless of:

  • The meaning and purpose of prayer
  • What prayer should do to their hearts and behavior
  • The connection between prayer and treating others with kindness
الَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَاءُونَ
"Those who make show [of their deeds]"
[Al-Ma'un 107:6]

Those who make show of their deeds. They pray for show, to be seen by others, not for Allah. Their worship is for people, not for Allah.

وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ
"And withhold [simple] assistance"
[Al-Ma'un 107:7]

And withhold simple assistance (الْمَاعُونَ - al-ma'un). They refuse to help others with even the simplest things - a pot, water, or basic assistance. They pray in the mosque but refuse to help their neighbors with basic needs.

The Duality Problem: This surah exposes a dangerous separation:

  • In the mosque: They pray and appear religious
  • Outside the mosque: They push away orphans, don't feed the poor, and refuse basic assistance
  • The problem: Religion is separated from daily life - this is the essence of hypocrisy

Key Lessons and Insights

1. Denying the Day of Judgment Leads to Harshness

Those who deny accountability in the Hereafter treat the weak and needy with contempt and harshness.

2. Prayer Must Transform Behavior

Prayer that doesn't change how we treat others is worthless. True prayer should make us more compassionate and helpful.

3. Religion Cannot Be Separated from Life

We cannot pray in the mosque and then mistreat people outside. Religion must be applied in all aspects of life.

4. Hypocrisy is a Grave Sin

Performing religious rituals for show while neglecting the rights of others is worse than not praying at all.

5. Simple Kindness is Required

We must help others with even the simplest things - a pot, water, or basic assistance. Refusing basic help shows a lack of faith.

Conclusion

Surah Al-Ma'un emphasizes that:

  • Those who deny the Day of Judgment treat orphans and the poor harshly
  • Prayer must transform our behavior - heedless prayer is worthless
  • We cannot separate religion from daily life
  • Hypocrisy - praying for show while mistreating others - is a grave sin
  • We must help others with even the simplest things
  • True faith requires both worship and good treatment of others

The surah calls believers to ensure their worship transforms their behavior, that they treat others with kindness and compassion, and that they never separate their religious practice from their daily interactions with people.

Source: This comprehensive guide is based on the tafsir (exegesis) lecture of Dr. Ahmed Abd al-Moneim from his course "Basa'ir Qur'aniyya" (Quranic Insights), specifically covering Surah Al-Ma'un.
