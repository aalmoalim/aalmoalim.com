Everything You Need to Know About Surah Az-Zalzalah (The Earthquake)

Introduction: Surah Az-Zalzalah describes the great earthquake that will occur on the Day of Judgment, when the earth will shake violently and reveal all the deeds that were done upon it. The surah emphasizes that every deed, no matter how small, will be brought forth and accounted for.

The Great Earthquake

إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا
"When the earth is shaken with its [final] earthquake"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:1]

When the earth is shaken with its final earthquake. This is the great earthquake that will occur on the Day of Judgment - the most violent earthquake that will ever occur.

وَأَخْرَجَتِ الْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا
"And the earth discharges its burdens"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:2]

And the earth discharges its burdens. The earth will cast out everything buried within it - the dead, treasures, and all that was hidden.

وَقَالَ الْإِنسَانُ مَا لَهَا
"And man says, 'What is [wrong] with it?'"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:3]

And man says, "What is wrong with it?" People will be astonished and confused by the violent shaking and what the earth is casting out.

The Great Earthquake: On the Day of Judgment:

  • The earth will shake with its final and most violent earthquake
  • The earth will discharge all its burdens - the dead, treasures, and hidden things
  • People will be astonished and confused by what is happening

The Earth Will Speak

يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا
"That Day, it will report its news"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:4]

That Day, it will report its news. The earth will speak and tell of everything that happened upon it - all the deeds, words, and actions that occurred on its surface.

بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا
"Because your Lord has commanded it"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:5]

Because your Lord has commanded it. The earth will speak because Allah has given it the ability and command to do so. Nothing happens without Allah's permission.

The Speaking Earth: On that Day:

  • The earth will speak and report all its news
  • It will tell of every deed, word, and action that occurred upon it
  • This happens because Allah has commanded it
  • Nothing can be hidden - everything will be revealed

Every Deed Will Be Brought Forth

يَوْمَئِذٍ يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا لِّيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ
"That Day, the people will depart separated [into categories] to be shown [the result of] their deeds"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:6]

That Day, the people will depart separated into categories to be shown the result of their deeds. People will be separated into groups - the righteous and the wicked - and each will see the consequences of their actions.

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ
"So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:7]

So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it. Every good deed, no matter how small, will be brought forth and rewarded.

وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ
"And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it"
[Az-Zalzalah 99:8]

And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it. Every evil deed, no matter how small, will be brought forth and accounted for.

Complete Accountability:

  • Every good deed, even an atom's weight, will be seen and rewarded
  • Every evil deed, even an atom's weight, will be seen and punished
  • Nothing is hidden or forgotten
  • Complete justice will be served

Key Lessons and Insights

1. The Great Earthquake Will Come

On the Day of Judgment, there will be a great earthquake that shakes the entire earth and discharges all its burdens.

2. The Earth Will Speak

The earth will speak and report everything that happened upon it. Nothing can be hidden.

3. Complete Accountability

Every deed, no matter how small, will be brought forth. An atom's weight of good will be rewarded, and an atom's weight of evil will be punished.

4. People Will Be Separated

People will be separated into categories - the righteous and the wicked - and each will see the results of their deeds.

5. Nothing Is Hidden

Everything that was done on earth will be revealed. The earth itself will testify to what occurred upon it.

Conclusion

Surah Az-Zalzalah emphasizes that:

  • On the Day of Judgment, there will be a great earthquake that shakes the entire earth
  • The earth will discharge all its burdens and speak of everything that happened upon it
  • Every deed, no matter how small, will be brought forth and accounted for
  • People will be separated and shown the results of their deeds
  • Complete justice will be served - nothing is hidden or forgotten

The surah calls believers to be mindful of every action, knowing that nothing is hidden and everything will be accounted for on the Day of Judgment.
