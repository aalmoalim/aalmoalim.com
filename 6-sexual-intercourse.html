Sexual Intercourse

The people of knowledge have unanimously agreed that whoever has intercourse during a day of Ramadan intentionally has disobeyed Allah, Glorified and Exalted, and His Messenger, peace and blessings be upon him, if he was a resident, and he had made the intention from the night. And upon him is the major expiation.

It is: freeing a slave, or fasting two consecutive months, or feeding sixty poor people.(1)

Due to the statement of Allah, the Exalted:

"It has been made lawful for you during the night of fasting to have sexual relations with your wives. They are a garment for you and you are a garment for them. Allah knows that you used to deceive yourselves, so He accepted your repentance and forgave you. So now, have relations with them and seek what Allah has ordained for you. And eat and drink until the white thread becomes distinct to you from the black thread of dawn, then complete the fast until night"
[Al-Baqarah 2:187]

And due to what Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated: While we were sitting with the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, a man came to him and said: O Messenger of Allah! I am ruined. He said: "What is the matter with you?" He said: I had intercourse with my wife while I was fasting. So the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Do you find a slave to free?" He said: No. He said: "Are you able to fast two consecutive months?" He said: No. So he said: "Do you find [means] to feed sixty poor people?" He said: No. He said: So the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, remained [silent]. While we were in that state, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, was brought a basket containing dates. Al-'araq is the basket. He said: "Where is the questioner?" So he said: I am. He said: "Take this and give it in charity." So the man said: To someone poorer than me, O Messenger of Allah? By Allah, there is no household between the two lava fields (meaning the two black fields) poorer than my household. So the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, laughed until his canines appeared, then he said: "Feed it to your family."(2)

Is Expiation Required in Sequence or as a Choice?

The jurists differed regarding expiation: is it in sequence or as a choice?

The Hanafis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis in the well-known view held that it is in sequence, like the expiation of zihar, due to the previous hadith of Abu Hurayrah, in which the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said to the one who had intercourse with his wife: "Do you find a slave to free?" He said: No. He said: "Are you able to fast two consecutive months?" He said: No. So he said: "Do you find [means] to feed sixty poor people?" He said: No. He said: So the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, remained [silent]. While we were in that state, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, was brought a basket containing dates... the hadith. So if he is able to free [a slave], he frees; if he does not find [one], then fasting two consecutive months; if he is not able, then feeding sixty poor people. And because the hadith is explicit in the issue.

The Malikis and Imam Ahmad in one narration held that expiation is a choice: either freeing a slave, or fasting two consecutive months, or feeding sixty poor people. And what is recommended according to the Malikis is feeding more than freeing and more than fasting, due to what Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated: "A man broke his fast in Ramadan, so the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, commanded him to expiate by freeing a slave, or fasting two consecutive months, or feeding sixty poor people."(3)

The wording "or" indicates choice. And because it is an expiation that was not required for destruction or excuse, so choice enters it, like the expiation of an oath.(4)

Ibn Rushd, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The reason for their difference regarding the obligation of sequence is the contradiction of the apparent meanings of the reports in that and the analogies. That is, the apparent meaning of the hadith of the Bedouin mentioned previously requires that it is in sequence, since the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, asked him about the ability for them in sequence. And the apparent meaning of what Malik narrated, that a man broke his fast in Ramadan, so the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, commanded him to free a slave, or fast two consecutive months, or feed sixty poor people, is that it is a choice, since "or" only indicates choice in the Arabic language, even if that was from the wording of the narrator companion, since they were the most knowledgeable of the understood meanings of situations and the indications of statements.

As for the opposing analogies in that, they compare it sometimes to the expiation of zihar, and sometimes to the expiation of an oath. But it is more similar to the expiation of zihar than to the expiation of an oath, and sequence was taken from the narration of the wording of the narrator.

As for the recommendation to begin with food, it contradicts the apparent meanings of the reports, and whoever held this did so by way of analogy, because he saw that fasting has occurred, and feeding has replaced it in various places in the Shari'ah, and that it is more appropriate for it than others, as evidence by the recitation of whoever recites: "And upon those who are able to bear it, a ransom of feeding a poor person" with the plural. And for this reason he and a group of scholars recommended that whoever dies and has fasting upon him, expiation should be made for him by feeding. And this is as if from the category of preferring analogy that the principles testify to over the report that the principles do not testify to.(5)

If One Repeats Intercourse in Ramadan, Is One Expiation Required or Not?

The jurists have unanimously agreed that whoever has intercourse during a day of Ramadan then expiates, then has intercourse on another day, another expiation is required upon him.(6)

And they differed regarding if one has intercourse during a day of Ramadan, then does not expiate until he has intercourse on another day:

The Malikis, Shafi'is, and Ahmad in the more correct of the two narrations from him held that two expiations are required upon him, because the cause of the obligation of expiation has been repeated, which is intercourse, and the ruling is repeated by the repetition of its cause, because each day is a separate act of worship, and he invalidated the fast of two days of Ramadan, so the obligation of an expiation in addition to the expiation of the first is required. And because they are two acts of worship, and the invalidation of one does not affect the other, like two Hajjs and two 'Umrahs.

The Hanafis and Abu Bakr from the Hanbalis, and it is the apparent choice of al-Khiraqi, held that only one expiation is required upon him, because it is a recompense for a transgression whose cause is repeated before it is fulfilled, so it must overlap, like the hadd.

And due to the hadith of the Bedouin mentioned previously, for when he said: I had intercourse with my wife, the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, commanded him to free one slave by his statement: "Free a slave," even though his statement "I had intercourse" may mean once or repeatedly, and he did not ask for clarification, so it indicates that the ruling does not differ by once or repeatedly.

Al-Kasani, may Allah have mercy on him, said: And because the meaning of deterrence is necessary in this expiation, I mean: the expiation of breaking the fast, as evidence by the specification of its obligation to the specific intentional act in the pure transgression that is free from doubt, unlike the rest of the expiations. And deterrence is achieved by one expiation, unlike if one has intercourse then expiates then has intercourse, because when he had intercourse after expiating, he knew that deterrence was not achieved by the first.(7)

Ibn Rushd, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The reason for their difference is comparing expiations to hadds. Whoever compared them to hadds said: One expiation suffices for that regarding many actions, just as the fornicator is required one flogging, even if he fornicates a thousand times, if he was not punished for one of them.

Whoever did not compare them to hadds made for each of the days a separate ruling by itself in violating the fast in it, requiring an expiation for each day.

They said: The difference between them is that expiation contains a type of act of worship, and hadds are pure deterrence.(8)

If One Repeats Intercourse Twice in a Day of Ramadan

The majority of jurists from the Hanafis, Malikis, and Shafi'is held that whoever has intercourse and expiates, then returns and has intercourse in his day a second time, a second expiation is not required upon him, and nothing is required upon him for that second intercourse, because it did not coincide with an established fast, unlike the first intercourse. And because it did not prevent its validity, so it did not require anything, like intercourse at night.

The Hanbalis held that a second expiation is required upon him, because fasting in Ramadan is an act of worship that requires expiation by intercourse in it, so it is repeated by the repetition of intercourse if it was after expiation, like Hajj. And because it is a forbidden intercourse due to the sanctity of Ramadan. So it requires expiation like the first.

Ibn Qudamah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: If it is said: The first intercourse included violating the fast, and it is effective in the obligation, so it is not correct to attach another to it.

We say: It is nullified by one upon whom dawn appeared while he is having intercourse and he continues in intercourse, for expiation is required upon him even though he did not violate the fast.(9)
