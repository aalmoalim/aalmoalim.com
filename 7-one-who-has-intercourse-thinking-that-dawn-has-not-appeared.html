Intercourse with Mistaken Timing

The jurists of the four schools have agreed that whoever has intercourse while thinking that the sun has set or that dawn has not appeared, then it appears contrary to his assumption, making up is obligatory upon him. Then they differed regarding expiation.

Abu Hanifah, Malik, and al-Shafi'i said: It is not required upon him.

Imam Ahmad said: Expiation is required upon him.(1)

(1) "Al-Ifsah" (1/404, 405), "Al-Mughni" (4/186), and "Al-Majmu'" (7/506, 543). The evidence for both views has preceded regarding one who eats or drinks while thinking that dawn has not appeared, and that the sun has set.
