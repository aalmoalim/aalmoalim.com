Third: Wet Dream

Ibn Hubayrah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: They have unanimously agreed that if the fasting person sleeps during a day of the month of Ramadan and has a wet dream in his sleep, his fast is not invalidated.(1)

Imam Al-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: If one has a wet dream, one does not break one's fast by consensus, because one is overcome, like one in whose stomach a fly falls without one's choice.(2)

And likewise if one has a wet dream at night and ritual bath is possible for one before dawn, and one does not perform ritual bath.

Al-Mawardi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The ummah has unanimously agreed that if one has a wet dream at night or has a wet dream during the day, one's fast is valid.(3) Due to the hadith of 'Aishah and Umm Salamah, may Allah be pleased with them both:

"The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, would be overtaken by dawn while in a state of major ritual impurity from his wife, then he would perform ritual bath and fast."(4)

(1) "Al-Ifsah" (1/409), and see: "Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah" (81).

(2) "Al-Majmu'" (7/509).

(3) "Al-Majmu'" (7/509).

(4) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1926) and Muslim (1109).
