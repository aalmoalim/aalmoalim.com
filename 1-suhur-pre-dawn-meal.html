Suhur (Pre-Dawn Meal)

The Ummah has unanimously agreed that suhur is recommended, it is mustahabb (recommended), and there is no sin upon one who leaves it, as said by Ibn al-Mundhir and others.(1)

Due to what is in the two Sahihs from Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Take suhur, for indeed in suhur there is blessing."(2)

Imam Ahmad narrated from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Suhur is a blessed meal, so do not leave it, even if one of you takes a gulp of water, for indeed Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, and His angels send blessings upon those who take suhur."(3)

Abu Dawud narrated from al-'Irbad ibn Sariyah, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: "The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, invited me to suhur in Ramadan, and said: 'Come to the blessed breakfast.'"(4)

Muslim narrated from 'Amr ibn al-'As, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"The distinction between our fasting and the fasting of the People of the Book is the meal of suhur."(5)

(1) "Al-Ijma'" (33), "Al-Majmu'" (7/605), and "Al-Mughni" (4/232).

(2) Narrated by Al-Bukhari (1923) and Muslim (1095).

(3) Hasan hadith: Narrated by Imam Ahmad (3/12, 44).

(4) Sahih hadith: Narrated by Abu Dawud (2344) and Al-Nasa'i (2163).

(5) Narrated by Muslim (1096).
