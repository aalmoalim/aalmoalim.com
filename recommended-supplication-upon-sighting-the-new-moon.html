Recommended Supplication Upon Sighting the New Moon

It is recommended to supplicate when sighting the new moon with what has been narrated by al-Tirmidhi from Talhah ibn 'Ubaydillah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, when he saw the new moon, would say:

“O Allah, bring it over us with blessing, faith, safety, and Islam. My Lord and your Lord is Allah.”(1)

And it was narrated by al-Darimi from Ibn 'Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, with the wording: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, when he saw the new moon, would say:

“Allah is Greatest. O Allah, bring it over us with security and faith, safety and Islam, and success in what our Lord loves and is pleased with. Our Lord and your Lord is Allah.”(2)

(1) Hasan hadith: Narrated by al-Tirmidhi (3451) and Ahmad (1/162).

(2) Sahih hadith: Narrated by al-Darimi (1687).
